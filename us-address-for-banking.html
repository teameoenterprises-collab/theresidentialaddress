The problem

Why your address gets an application declined

Virtual mailboxes, UPS Store boxes and most mail centers are registered with the USPS as Commercial Mail Receiving Agencies (CMRAs). Banks, fintechs and payment processors see that flag the moment you submit, because it is not where anyone lives or runs a business.

A PO box is not a street address at all, and a registered agent address is shared by thousands of companies. That is why so many founders get stuck at the address step, even with a valid LLC, EIN and passport.

Questions

US address for banking, answered

Why do US banks reject virtual mailbox addresses?

Virtual mailboxes, UPS Store boxes and most mail centers are registered with the USPS as Commercial Mail Receiving Agencies (CMRAs). Banks and payment processors can see that flag instantly, and many decline a CMRA as a company or personal address because it is not where anyone lives or works.

Is this a real residential address?

Yes. It is a residential unit in the US, held under a signed lease, and it is not registered as a CMRA. You also receive renter's insurance documents for the address.

Which accounts can I use the address for?

Founders use it as the address on US business bank and fintech applications such as Mercury, on payment processors such as Stripe, and for IRS and state correspondence. Each provider sets its own rules, so check the application requirements of the bank you choose. See our guides on opening a US bank account as a non-resident and Stripe for non-US LLC owners.

How much does it cost?

$2,000 per year, billed annually, with no setup fee. It includes the residential address, the signed lease, renter's insurance documents and mail handling.

How fast can I start using it?

Most founders have their address and documents within 1 to 2 business days of signing up.

Can I use a registered agent address for my bank account instead?

A registered agent address receives legal notices for your LLC. It is shared by thousands of companies, so banks rarely accept it as your business or personal address. More in registered agent vs business address.
