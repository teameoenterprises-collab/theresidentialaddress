How Australian Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇦🇺🇺🇸🌏

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes. An Australian resident can form and own a US LLC without US citizenship or residency, and the US and Australia have an income tax treaty in force. On the Australian side, ATO guidance treats a US LLC as a company unless the foreign hybrid rules in Division 830 apply, which can treat it as a partnership if specific conditions are met. Because the outcome turns on those conditions, get advice from an Australian tax adviser who works with US entities.

Can Australians own a US LLC?

Yes. No US state requires LLC members to live in the US or hold a US visa. From Sydney, Melbourne or Perth you can file the state formation document online, appoint a registered agent in that state and apply for an EIN. The time difference with the US is often a bigger practical hurdle than any legal rule, especially for phone calls with the IRS or banks.

Is there a US-Australia tax treaty?

Yes. Australia is on the IRS list of countries with which the US has an income tax treaty. How it applies to income earned through an LLC depends on how each country classifies the LLC and its income, so confirm treaty treatment with an adviser rather than assuming it.

How the ATO treats a US LLC

ATO guidance uses the term foreign hybrid for an entity that is taxed in Australia as a company but taxed overseas as a partnership, and it names US limited liability companies as an example.

  • The starting point is that Australia treats a US LLC as a company.
  • Division 830 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 can treat a foreign hybrid as a partnership for Australian tax instead, so members are taxed on their share, if certain conditions are met.
  • Whether those conditions are met for your LLC, including a single-member LLC that the IRS disregards rather than taxes as a partnership, is a technical question for your adviser.

If the US and Australia end up treating the LLC differently, each may tax different persons or tax the income at different times, which can make foreign tax offsets harder to match.

US-side filings

  1. Get an EIN. Without an SSN or ITIN you cannot use the IRS online application. Apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail, or by phone as an international applicant. See EIN without an SSN.
  2. File Form 5472 each year it applies. A single-member LLC owned by a non-US person attaches it to a pro forma Form 1120 in any year with reportable transactions, such as funding or withdrawals. The penalty for not filing is $25,000. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  3. Skip BOI. FinCEN's final rule effective August 14, 2026 exempts LLCs formed in a US state from beneficial ownership reporting. See BOI for non-resident LLCs.
  4. Apply for an ITIN only if needed. It is not required to form the LLC or get an EIN. See ITIN guide.

Banking and payments from Australia

US banks and fintech providers set their own rules for LLCs with owners abroad. Some accept Australian-resident owners online, others require a branch visit or a US resident owner. Check the provider's eligibility page first, and see opening a US business bank account as a non-resident.

  • Have your Articles of Organization, EIN letter, operating agreement and passport ready.
  • Use your real Australian home address wherever a bank asks for your personal residential address.
  • Expect many banks to decline mailbox-store or virtual mailbox business addresses. See CMRA addresses.

Stripe operates in Australia, so an Australian business can take card payments locally. An LLC that wants US dollar payouts in its own name can open a US Stripe account. See Stripe for non-US LLC owners.

Which state Australians usually choose

Wyoming is a frequent choice for non-resident owners because of its $100 state filing fee, $60 minimum annual license tax and no state income tax. Delaware is more common when US venture investment is planned. See Wyoming LLC for non-residents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an Australian resident form a US LLC?

Yes. US states have no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a registered agent in the state and an EIN from the IRS.

Does Australia tax a US LLC as a company or a partnership?

ATO guidance treats a US LLC as a company unless the Division 830 foreign hybrid rules apply, in which case it can be treated as a partnership. Whether they apply depends on specific conditions.

Is there a US-Australia tax treaty?

Yes. Australia is on the IRS list of countries with a US income tax treaty in force.

Do Australian owners file Form 5472?

Generally yes, if the LLC is a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person and has reportable transactions with its owner during the year.
