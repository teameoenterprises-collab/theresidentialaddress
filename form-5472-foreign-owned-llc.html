Form 5472 for Foreign-Owned Single-Member LLCs Explained

📑⚖️📆

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: If a non-US person owns a single-member US LLC, the LLC generally has to file IRS Form 5472, attached to a pro forma Form 1120, every year it has a reportable transaction with its owner. Putting money into the LLC or taking money out counts. The return is due April 15 for calendar-year LLCs, and the penalty for not filing is $25,000 per form, per year.

Who has to file

Since tax years beginning in 2017, a US single-member LLC wholly owned by a foreign person is treated as a corporation for Form 5472 purposes, even though it is otherwise a "disregarded entity" for income tax. That means most non-resident founders with a one-owner LLC have this filing every year, whether or not the LLC made money.

What counts as a reportable transaction

  • Money you put into the LLC, including the first deposit into its bank account.
  • Money you take out (distributions).
  • Loans between you and the LLC.
  • Formation costs or expenses you paid personally on the LLC's behalf.
  • Payments between the LLC and other companies you own.

In practice almost every active foreign-owned LLC has at least one of these each year.

How it is filed

  • Fill in Form 5472 for each related party.
  • Attach it to a pro forma Form 1120 with only the company's identifying details completed.
  • Most filers fax or mail it to the IRS. It cannot be filed through ordinary e-file software in most cases.
  • Deadline: April 15 for a calendar-year LLC. An extension (Form 7004) moves it to October 15.

The penalty

The penalty is $25,000 for each form not filed, or filed incomplete, plus further penalties if it is still not filed after the IRS sends a notice. There is no statutory cap. The IRS does not send reminders.

Form 5472 is separate from BOI reporting

US-formed LLCs no longer file FinCEN beneficial ownership (BOI) reports, but that change did not affect Form 5472. See BOI reporting for non-resident LLCs in 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to file Form 5472 if my LLC had no income?

Usually yes. The requirement depends on reportable transactions with related parties, such as funding the LLC or withdrawing money, not on income.

What is the penalty for not filing Form 5472?

$25,000 per form, per year, with additional penalties if it is still not filed after IRS notice.

When is Form 5472 due?

April 15 for a calendar-year foreign-owned single-member LLC, or October 15 with a Form 7004 extension.

Can I e-file Form 5472 for a single-member LLC?

Generally no. Foreign-owned single-member LLCs usually fax or mail Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120.
