How Nigerian Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇳🇬🏢💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes. A founder living in Nigeria can form and own a US LLC without US citizenship, a visa or US residency. There is no US income tax treaty with Nigeria in force, so no treaty reductions are available. Once formed, the LLC needs an EIN, files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year if you are the only owner, and does not file a FinCEN BOI report. At home, Nigeria's 2025 tax reforms and Central Bank of Nigeria foreign exchange rules still apply to you.

Can Nigerian residents own a US LLC?

Yes. US states do not require LLC owners to be US citizens or residents. Nigeria is not one of the countries covered by a comprehensive US sanctions program on the OFAC sanctions programs list, so ordinary residents can form a company, although every owner is still screened by banks and payment providers. If you want the general background first, read can you open a US LLC without living in America.

Is there a US tax treaty with Nigeria?

No. Nigeria does not appear on the IRS list of US income tax treaties. In practice this means:

  • You cannot claim reduced US withholding rates under a treaty, for example on US dividends or interest paid to you personally.
  • Whether the LLC's profit is taxed in the US at all depends on whether it is income effectively connected with a US trade or business. The IRS explains the test on its effectively connected income page. Many founders who work entirely from Lagos or Abuja for overseas clients have little or no US income tax, but this is a fact-specific question for a US tax adviser.

US filings for a Nigerian-owned LLC

  • EIN: You get the company's tax ID by sending Form SS-4 to the IRS by fax or mail. No SSN is needed. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472: A single-member LLC owned by a foreign person files Form 5472 attached to a pro forma Form 1120 for each year it has reportable transactions with you, such as your first deposit. It is due April 15 for a calendar year and the IRS penalty for not filing is $25,000. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • BOI: FinCEN's final rule, effective August 14, 2026, permanently exempts companies formed in a US state from beneficial ownership reporting. See BOI reporting in 2026.
  • ITIN: You do not need one to form the LLC or get the EIN. You may need one later to file a personal US return or when a bank or processor asks for a US taxpayer number. See how to get an ITIN as a non-resident.

The Nigerian side

Nigeria's tax system changed on January 1, 2026, when four reform acts signed on June 26, 2025 took effect, including the Nigeria Tax Act and the act that turned the Federal Inland Revenue Service into the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS). A diaspora guide published on the Nigerians in Diaspora Commission website summarises the residence rule this way: if you spend 183 days or more in Nigeria in a tax year you are a tax resident and are taxed on income earned worldwide, subject to double taxation agreements, with credit for foreign tax paid. Profit from a US LLC you own is therefore likely to be relevant to your Nigerian return.

Foreign exchange is a separate layer. The Central Bank of Nigeria's Foreign Exchange Manual (4th edition) governs how money moves in and out of Nigeria through authorised dealer banks, including documented forms such as Form A for payments for services. Keep records of every transfer between your Nigerian accounts and the LLC. Confirm how your LLC income, any transfers and your filing status apply to you with a Nigerian tax adviser before you start trading.

Banking and payments

A US LLC can hold a US business account, but each bank and fintech sets its own list of supported countries of residence, and those lists change. Check a provider's eligibility page before you apply rather than relying on older forum posts. See opening a US business bank account as a non-resident.

  • Expect to provide your passport, the LLC's formation documents, the EIN letter and proof of your residential address in Nigeria. Always give your real home address as your personal address.
  • Many banks and Stripe question or decline a virtual mailbox (CMRA) as the company's address. See what a CMRA address is.
  • Stripe accepts US-registered businesses owned from abroad, subject to verification of the company, its EIN and the representative. See Stripe for non-US LLC owners.

Which state do Nigerian founders usually pick?

Wyoming is a common choice for small, owner-run businesses because of its $100 filing fee, $60 minimum annual report tax and no state income tax. Delaware is usually preferred if you plan to raise money from US investors. Compare them in Wyoming vs Delaware or read the Wyoming LLC guide for non-residents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Nigerian citizen living in Nigeria form a US LLC?

Yes. US states do not require LLC owners to be US citizens or residents, and Nigeria is not under comprehensive US sanctions. You need a registered agent in the state you choose.

Is there a US-Nigeria tax treaty?

No. Nigeria is not on the IRS list of US income tax treaties, so treaty-reduced US withholding rates are not available to Nigerian residents.

Do I need an SSN or ITIN to get an EIN for my LLC?

No. A non-resident can get an EIN by sending Form SS-4 to the IRS by fax or mail without an SSN or ITIN.

Will Nigeria tax my US LLC income?

Possibly. Under the 2025 reforms effective January 1, 2026, Nigerian tax residents are generally taxed on worldwide income, with relief for foreign tax. Confirm your position with a Nigerian tax adviser.
