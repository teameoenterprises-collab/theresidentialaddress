How Mexican Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇲🇽🇺🇸📊

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes. A Mexican resident can form and own a US LLC without US citizenship or residency, and the US and Mexico have an income tax treaty in force. Mexico's Income Tax Law (Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta) has specific rules for transparent foreign entities: Mexican residents pay tax on income earned through them in proportion to their share. Because a single-member LLC can fall under those rules, plan with a Mexican tax adviser.

Can a Mexican resident own a US LLC?

Yes. No US state requires LLC members to be US citizens or to live in the US. From Mexico City, Guadalajara or Monterrey you file the formation document with the state online, appoint a registered agent there and apply for an EIN. Mexican founders often use a US LLC to sell to US buyers, contract with US companies or receive US dollar payments in a US entity's name.

Is there a US-Mexico tax treaty?

Yes. Mexico is on the IRS list of countries with which the US has an income tax treaty. Mexico's own law also says its transparency rules for foreign entities give way to tax treaty provisions where a treaty applies. How the treaty works for your LLC depends on the type of income and how the LLC is classified, so confirm it with an adviser.

How Mexico treats a US LLC

Articles 4-A and 4-B of Mexico's Income Tax Law deal with foreign entities that are fiscally transparent:

  • A foreign entity is considered transparent when it is not a tax resident where it was formed or where it is managed, and its income is attributed to its members. A single-member LLC that the IRS disregards may fit this description.
  • Mexican residents must pay tax on income obtained through transparent foreign entities in proportion to their participation, generally measured by the entity's tax profit for the calendar year.
  • If a foreign entity has its principal administration or place of effective management in Mexico, the law can treat it as a Mexican tax resident instead. Running the LLC day to day from Mexico can therefore matter.
  • Article 4-A states that it does not apply to tax treaties, whose provisions apply instead.

The practical result is that profits of your LLC may be taxable to you in Mexico in the year they are earned, not only when you withdraw them. Ask your contador or tax adviser how this applies to your LLC and what you must report to the SAT.

US paperwork for Mexican owners

  1. EIN. Without an SSN or ITIN you cannot use the IRS online form. File Form SS-4 by fax or mail, or apply by phone as an international applicant. See EIN without an SSN.
  2. Form 5472 and pro forma 1120. Generally required each year a foreign-owned single-member LLC has reportable transactions with its owner, including the money you use to open the bank account. The penalty for not filing is $25,000. See Form 5472.
  3. No BOI report. LLCs formed in a US state are exempt under FinCEN's final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI for non-resident LLCs.
  4. ITIN if needed. Not required for formation or the EIN, but needed if you have to file a US personal return. See how to get an ITIN.

Banking and payments from Mexico

Some US banks and fintech providers serve LLCs owned by Mexican residents, some require a branch visit and some limit service to US residents. Check the provider's current eligibility page, and see US business bank accounts for non-residents.

  • Prepare the Articles of Organization, EIN letter, operating agreement and your passport.
  • List your real home address in Mexico as your personal residential address.
  • Many banks decline a business address at a mailbox store or virtual mailbox. See CMRA addresses.

Stripe supports businesses in Mexico, so a Mexican business can accept payments without a US entity. If the LLC should receive US dollar payouts in its own name, it can apply for a US Stripe account. See Stripe for non-US LLC owners.

Which state Mexican founders pick

Wyoming and Delaware are the usual choices for non-resident founders. Wyoming offers low yearly costs and no state income tax, and Delaware is familiar to US investors. A founder who will run warehouses, staff or an office in a border state such as Texas may also need to register there. See Wyoming LLC for non-residents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Mexican citizen living in Mexico form a US LLC?

Yes. US states have no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a registered agent in the state and an EIN from the IRS.

Is there a tax treaty between the US and Mexico?

Yes. Mexico is on the IRS list of countries with a US income tax treaty in force.

Will Mexico tax my US LLC's profits?

Possibly in the year they are earned. Articles 4-A and 4-B of Mexico's Income Tax Law make Mexican residents pay tax on income obtained through transparent foreign entities in proportion to their share. Ask a Mexican adviser how this applies to you.

Does a Mexican-owned single-member LLC file Form 5472?

Generally yes, in any year with reportable transactions between the LLC and its foreign owner, such as funding or withdrawals.
