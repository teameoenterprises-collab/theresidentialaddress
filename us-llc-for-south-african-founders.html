How South African Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇿🇦📈🏦

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes, founders who live in South Africa can set up a US LLC entirely from Johannesburg, Cape Town or anywhere else, with no US visa or residency. The US and South Africa have an income tax treaty, signed in 1997 with a general effective date of 1 January 1998. After formation you need an EIN, an annual Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 if you are the sole owner, and no FinCEN BOI report. At home, SARS taxes South African residents on their worldwide income, so the LLC's profit still needs to be planned for there.

Who can form the LLC

Any adult can be the owner, or member, of a US LLC. State law does not ask about citizenship or where you live. South Africa is not the subject of a comprehensive US sanctions program on the OFAC sanctions programs page, so the practical limits come from banks, payment processors and marketplaces, which each decide which countries they serve.

What you need to get started:

  • A valid passport.
  • A registered agent with a physical address in your formation state.
  • A company name that is available in that state.
  • An operating agreement, which banks commonly ask to see.

The US-South Africa tax treaty

South Africa is on the IRS list of US income tax treaties. The treaty text was signed in Cape Town on 17 February 1997. Points founders ask about most:

  • Business profits (Article 7): profits of a South African enterprise are taxable only in South Africa unless it carries on business in the US through a permanent establishment there.
  • Dividends (Article 10): US tax is capped at 5% for a company owning at least 10% of the voting stock, and 15% in other cases.
  • Interest and royalties (Articles 11 and 12): generally taxable only in the country where the beneficial owner lives, subject to the treaty's anti-abuse rules in Article 22.

Whether these benefits reach you through a single-member LLC depends on how each country treats the LLC, because the IRS ignores a single-member LLC for income tax unless it elects otherwise. A position that reduces US tax under a treaty generally has to be disclosed on Form 8833. Ask a cross-border tax adviser to check your actual setup before you rely on the treaty.

Your IRS checklist

  1. EIN. Fill in Form SS-4, write "foreign" on line 7b if you have no SSN or ITIN, and apply by fax, mail or the IRS international phone line. See getting an EIN without an SSN.
  2. Form 5472 each year. Required for a foreign-owned single-member LLC with reportable transactions, such as you funding the company or taking money out. It goes with a pro forma Form 1120 and is due April 15 for calendar-year companies. Details in Form 5472 explained.
  3. No BOI report. A FinCEN final rule effective August 14, 2026 made the exemption for US-formed companies permanent. See BOI for non-resident LLCs.
  4. ITIN only if needed. An ITIN is your personal US tax number, used when you must file a US personal return or claim certain treaty benefits. See ITIN vs EIN.

South African tax obligations

The South African Revenue Service says South Africa has a residence-based system: residents are taxed on their worldwide income, wherever it is earned, while non-residents are taxed only on South African-source income. SARS also says foreign taxes are offset against South African tax payable on foreign income. In practice:

  • Owning a US LLC does not take its profit outside the South African tax net if you are a South African tax resident.
  • How the LLC's profit and your withdrawals appear on your return depends on how South African law classifies the LLC, which is technical. Confirm the treatment with a South African tax adviser before your first filing season.
  • Moving money between South Africa and a US account can involve exchange control rules, so speak to your South African bank before you fund the LLC.

Bank accounts, Stripe and addresses

US business accounts are available to many non-resident LLC owners, but supported countries differ by provider and change without notice. Read each provider's current eligibility rules and start with our guide to US business banking for non-residents.

  • Your personal address on any application should be where you actually live in South Africa, with matching proof such as a utility bill or bank statement.
  • Virtual mailbox addresses, known as CMRAs, are often flagged or declined as a business address by banks and processors. See what is a CMRA address.
  • Stripe supports US-registered companies with foreign owners and verifies the LLC's EIN, legal name and representative. See Stripe for a non-US LLC owner.

Picking a state

Most South African founders running a lean online business look at Wyoming first: a $100 state filing fee, a $60 minimum annual report tax and no state income tax. Founders planning to raise from US venture investors usually choose Delaware. Read Wyoming LLC for non-residents for the steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I form a US LLC while living in South Africa?

Yes. There is no US citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners, and South Africa is not under comprehensive US sanctions.

Does the US have a tax treaty with South Africa?

Yes. The treaty was signed on 17 February 1997 and has a general effective date of 1 January 1998. It covers business profits, dividends, interest and royalties, among other income.

Does SARS tax my US LLC's profit?

SARS taxes South African residents on worldwide income. How the LLC's profit and your withdrawals are reported depends on how South African law classifies the LLC, so confirm with a South African tax adviser.

Does my LLC have to file with the IRS if it made no money?

Usually yes. A foreign-owned single-member LLC generally files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 for any year with reportable transactions, such as funding the company, even with no income.
