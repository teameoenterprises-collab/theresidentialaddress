US LLC for UAE Residents: How It Works

🇦🇪🇺🇸🏢

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes, a UAE resident of any nationality can own a US LLC without living in the US. You form it in a US state, get an EIN from the IRS, and open a US bank account. Two points matter more for UAE residents than for most founders: the US has no income tax treaty with the UAE, and the UAE's own 9% corporate tax may apply depending on how and where the business is run.

Why UAE residents form US LLCs

  • To sell to US customers and get paid in US dollars.
  • To open a US business bank account and use US payment processors and marketplaces.
  • To keep a US business separate from a UAE free zone or mainland company.

Note that Stripe has operated in the UAE since 2021 for businesses based there, so a US LLC is not the only way to accept card payments. Choose the US LLC because you need a US company, not only for payments.

Step 1: Choose a state and form the LLC

You do not need a US visa, SSN or personal US address to form an LLC. You do need a registered agent with a physical address in the state. Most non-resident founders compare Wyoming and Delaware; see Wyoming vs Delaware LLC and Wyoming LLC for non-residents. Check the Secretary of State website for the current formation and annual report fees in your chosen state.

Step 2: Get an EIN

Without an SSN or ITIN, you apply for the LLC's EIN on IRS Form SS-4 by fax or mail, or by phone from outside the US. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.

Step 3: Banking

Online banks and fintechs that accept US-formed companies with owners abroad are the usual route. They ask for formation documents, the EIN letter, a passport and the owner's real home address, which for you is your UAE address. Some banks restrict certain countries or industries, so read each bank's eligibility page. See opening a US business bank account as a non-resident and Mercury's address requirements.

Step 4: US tax filings

  • Form 5472. A US single-member LLC wholly owned by a foreign person files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 every year it has reportable transactions with its owner, such as capital contributions or distributions. The penalty for not filing is $25,000. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • US income tax. A single-member LLC owned by a non-US person is usually a disregarded entity. Whether it owes US federal income tax depends on whether it has income effectively connected with a US trade or business, for example US staff, a US office or US inventory.
  • No US-UAE tax treaty. The IRS list of US income tax treaties does not include the UAE. Treaty benefits such as reduced withholding or the permanent establishment test are not available, so US-source income is taxed under ordinary US rules.
  • BOI. US-formed LLCs no longer file FinCEN beneficial ownership reports. See BOI reporting for non-resident LLCs.

Step 5: How UAE corporate tax fits in

UAE Corporate Tax applies from the first financial year starting on or after 1 June 2023, under Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022. The rates are 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above that. It applies to businesses and to individuals doing business under a UAE commercial licence, and to foreign entities that carry on business in the UAE in an ongoing or regular manner.

If you run a US LLC from the UAE, ask a UAE tax adviser whether its profits fall into UAE Corporate Tax, either through your licensed UAE business or because the LLC is managed from the UAE. Keep the LLC's records and decisions clearly documented either way.

Do you need an ITIN?

Not to form the LLC or get its EIN. An ITIN is needed if you must file a US personal tax return, and some payment processors ask for one. See how to get an ITIN as a non-resident.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a UAE resident open a US LLC?

Yes. Owning a US LLC has no US citizenship or residency requirement. You need a registered agent in the state of formation, and the LLC needs an EIN from the IRS.

Is there a tax treaty between the US and the UAE?

No. The IRS list of US income tax treaties does not include the United Arab Emirates, so treaty benefits are not available and ordinary US rules apply to US-source income.

Does UAE corporate tax apply to my US LLC?

It can. UAE Corporate Tax is 0% up to AED 375,000 of taxable income and 9% above, and applies to UAE businesses and to foreign entities doing business in the UAE regularly. Ask a UAE tax adviser how it applies to your setup.

Does a UAE-owned US LLC file Form 5472?

A single-member US LLC wholly owned by a foreign person generally files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year it has reportable transactions with its owner. The penalty for not filing is $25,000.

Do I need a US address to form an LLC from the UAE?

You need a registered agent address in the state of formation. Banks and payment processors also ask for a business address and your real home address, which can be in the UAE.
