How to Form a Pennsylvania LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🔔📄💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Anyone can form a Pennsylvania LLC from abroad, with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file a certificate of organization with the Pennsylvania Department of State for $125 and list a registered office, which must be a Pennsylvania street address or a Commercial Registered Office Provider (CROP). From the year after formation, the LLC files a $7 annual report by September 30. Starting with reports due in 2027, missing it can lead to administrative dissolution.

What Pennsylvania charges

The Department of State publishes its fees on its fees and payments page.

  • Certificate of organization: $125, paid once.
  • Annual report: $7 a year for an LLC, due January 1 to September 30.
  • Change of registered office: $5.
  • Reinstatement after dissolution: $35 filed online, $40 on paper, plus $15 for each annual report not filed.
  • Registering an LLC formed in another state: $250.
  • Expedited service: optional, from $100 for same-day up to $1,000 for one-hour service, on top of the filing fee.
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

Department fees are nonrefundable. CROP fees are extra and set by each provider.

Step by step

  1. Check the name in the Pennsylvania business search.
  2. Choose a registered office. It must be an actual street address in Pennsylvania, not a post office box alone. If you have no location in the state, you can list a CROP, after signing a contract with it.
  3. File the certificate of organization online or on paper, with the $125 fee.
  4. Sign an operating agreement. It is kept by the company, not filed, and banks often ask for it.
  5. Get an EIN with Form SS-4, then open a bank account.
  6. Diarise the annual report for September 30 of the year after you form.

The registered office and CROPs

Pennsylvania works differently from most states. It does not require a registered agent by name. Instead, every LLC lists a registered office in the state. The Department says a CROP is an option for any association with no physical location or mailing address in Pennsylvania, which covers most founders abroad.

  • You should have a contract with the CROP before listing it. Listing a CROP without one can expose the filer to civil and criminal penalties.
  • A CROP cannot be used where a principal place of business is required, such as a fictitious name registration.
  • A CROP receives legal and state mail. It is not a business address for banking, and many CROPs do not forward everyday mail.

The annual report

Pennsylvania introduced annual reports in 2025. For LLCs, domestic and foreign, the filing window is January 1 to September 30, and the first report is due in the year after formation. An LLC formed in 2026 first files by September 30, 2027.

The report is filed online at the Department's filing site and asks for:

  • The LLC's name, entity number and jurisdiction of formation.
  • The registered office address and the principal office address.
  • The name of at least one governor, which for an LLC means a manager or a member with material management responsibility.
  • Names and titles of principal officers, if any.

No financial information is required. Starting with reports due in 2027, an LLC that does not file is subject to administrative dissolution six months after the due date. A dissolved domestic LLC can apply for reinstatement with no time limit by paying the reinstatement fee and each missed report.

Privacy: what is public

The annual report puts the name of at least one manager or managing member on the public record each year. If keeping your name off state filings matters, compare states that ask for less, such as New Mexico or Ohio.

Federal steps for non-resident owners

These apply to every US LLC, whatever the state.

  • EIN: without an SSN or ITIN you apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472: a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year, due April 15, even with no income. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • BOI: LLCs formed in the US are exempt from FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting under the final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.

Pennsylvania taxes to keep in mind

The $7 annual report is a filing fee, not a tax. Pennsylvania has state and local taxes that can apply to business activity in the state, depending on where your sales, staff and property actually are. Check the Pennsylvania Department of Revenue's guidance or ask a Pennsylvania tax adviser before you rely on the LLC owing nothing there.

Registered office vs business address

The registered office is where the state and courts send legal notices. It is not automatically your business or mailing address.

Banks and payment providers usually ask for a business address and for each owner's real personal residential address, which for most non-residents is their home abroad. Many banks decline CMRA or virtual mailbox addresses as a business address, so check the bank's eligibility page before you apply. See what a CMRA address is.

Pennsylvania vs Wyoming or Delaware

Pennsylvania's $125 formation fee and $7 annual report keep state costs low, and the CROP system suits founders abroad. It fits best if you have customers, staff or inventory in Pennsylvania. For a remote online business with no Pennsylvania connection, compare Wyoming and Wyoming vs Delaware first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form a Pennsylvania LLC?

Yes. Pennsylvania has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a registered office, which can be a Pennsylvania street address or a Commercial Registered Office Provider.

How much does a Pennsylvania LLC cost?

The certificate of organization is $125 and the annual report is $7 a year. CROP fees are extra.

When is the Pennsylvania LLC annual report due?

Between January 1 and September 30 each year, starting the year after formation. An LLC formed in 2026 first files by September 30, 2027.

What happens if I miss the annual report?

Starting with reports due in 2027, a domestic LLC that does not file is subject to administrative dissolution six months after the due date. It can be reinstated for $35 online plus $15 for each missed report.

Does Pennsylvania require a registered agent?

No. It requires a registered office in the state. Founders with no Pennsylvania location usually list a Commercial Registered Office Provider under a contract.
