How Ghanaian Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇬🇭📈🏦

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes, founders who live in Ghana can set up a US LLC entirely from Accra, Kumasi or anywhere else, with no US visa or residency. There is no income tax treaty between the US and Ghana, so treaty rate reductions are not available. After formation you need an EIN, an annual Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 if you are the sole owner, and no FinCEN BOI report. At home, the Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) taxes resident persons on worldwide income, with a deduction for foreign tax credit.

Who can form the LLC

Any adult can be the owner, or member, of a US LLC. State law does not ask about citizenship or where you live. In practice the limits come from banks, payment processors and marketplaces, which each decide which countries they serve.

What you need to get started:

  • A valid passport.
  • A registered agent with a physical address in your formation state.
  • A company name that is available in that state.
  • An operating agreement, which banks commonly ask to see.

No US-Ghana tax treaty

Ghana does not appear on the IRS list of US income tax treaties, and the United States is not among the countries the GRA lists as having a double taxation treaty with Ghana. What this means for founders:

  • US-source income paid to you, such as dividends from a US corporation, is taxed at the standard US rates for non-residents, with no treaty reduction.
  • There is no treaty "permanent establishment" test. Whether the LLC's profit is US taxable depends on US domestic rules, mainly whether it is income effectively connected with a US trade or business.
  • A single-member LLC is ignored by the IRS for income tax unless it elects otherwise, so its income is treated as yours.

Many lean online businesses run from Ghana, with no US office, staff or inventory, have little or no US income tax, but this depends on the facts. Ask a cross-border tax adviser to check your setup.

Your IRS checklist

  1. EIN. Fill in Form SS-4, write "foreign" on line 7b if you have no SSN or ITIN, and apply by fax, mail or the IRS international phone line. See getting an EIN without an SSN.
  2. Form 5472 each year. Required for a foreign-owned single-member LLC with reportable transactions, such as you funding the company or taking money out. It goes with a pro forma Form 1120 and is due April 15 for calendar-year companies. Details in Form 5472 explained.
  3. No BOI report. A FinCEN final rule effective August 14, 2026 made the exemption for US-formed companies permanent. See BOI for non-resident LLCs.
  4. ITIN only if needed. An ITIN is your personal US tax number, used when you must file a US personal return. See ITIN vs EIN.

Ghanaian tax obligations

The GRA's personal income tax guidance says a resident person's assessable income is their worldwide income from all sources, and that a deduction is allowed for foreign tax credit. Its domestic tax FAQ confirms that the foreign income of a resident person is taxable, with limited exceptions for some foreign employment income.

In an April 2024 statement citing the Income Tax Act, 2015 (Act 896), the GRA said you are resident for tax purposes if, among other tests, you are present in Ghana for at least 183 days in any 12-month period that begins or ends in the year, or you are a citizen with a permanent home in Ghana. In practice:

  • Owning a US LLC does not take its profit outside the Ghanaian tax net if you are a Ghanaian tax resident.
  • Any US tax you pay may be credited against Ghanaian tax on the same income, within the GRA's rules.
  • Rules for moving money into and out of Ghana can affect how you fund the LLC and bring profits home, so speak to your Ghanaian bank first.

Confirm how the LLC's profit and your withdrawals should appear on your Ghanaian return with a Ghanaian tax adviser before your first filing season.

Bank accounts, Stripe and addresses

US business accounts are available to many non-resident LLC owners, but supported countries differ by provider and change without notice. Read each provider's current eligibility rules and start with our guide to US business banking for non-residents.

  • Your personal address on any application should be where you actually live in Ghana, with matching proof such as a utility bill or bank statement.
  • Virtual mailbox addresses, known as CMRAs, are often flagged or declined as a business address by banks and processors. See what is a CMRA address.
  • Stripe supports US-registered companies with foreign owners and verifies the LLC's EIN, legal name and representative. See Stripe for a non-US LLC owner.

Picking a state

Most founders running a lean online business look at Wyoming first: a $100 state filing fee, a $60 minimum annual report tax and no state income tax. Founders planning to raise from US venture investors usually choose Delaware. Read Wyoming LLC for non-residents for the steps, or compare with founders in Nigeria and Kenya.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I form a US LLC while living in Ghana?

Yes. There is no US citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. Banks and payment processors set their own country rules, so check each provider's eligibility page.

Does the US have a tax treaty with Ghana?

No. Ghana is not on the IRS list of US income tax treaties, and the US is not on the GRA's list of Ghana's double taxation treaties.

Does the GRA tax my US LLC's profit?

If you are a Ghanaian tax resident, the GRA taxes your worldwide income from all sources and allows a deduction for foreign tax credit. Confirm the treatment of LLC profits with a Ghanaian tax adviser.

Does my LLC have to file with the IRS if it made no money?

Usually yes. A foreign-owned single-member LLC generally files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 for any year with reportable transactions, such as funding the company, even with no income.
