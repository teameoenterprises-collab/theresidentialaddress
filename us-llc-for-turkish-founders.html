How Turkish Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇹🇷🏢🌉

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes. A founder living in Turkey can form and own a US LLC with no US citizenship, visa or residency. The US and Turkey have an income tax treaty in force, which can matter for income you receive personally from the US. The LLC itself needs an EIN, files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year if you are the only owner, and does not file a FinCEN BOI report. Turkish tax reporting is a separate step to confirm with a local adviser.

Eligibility

LLC statutes in Wyoming, Delaware and other states set no citizenship or residency requirement for members. Turkey is not covered by a comprehensive US sanctions program on the OFAC sanctions programs list, so a resident of Istanbul, Ankara or Izmir can form an LLC in the usual way. Providers still screen every owner and decide for themselves which countries they serve.

The US-Turkey tax treaty

Turkey appears on the IRS list of US income tax treaties, and the IRS publishes the 1996 treaty and its technical explanation. How it may matter:

  • Because a single-member LLC is normally disregarded for US income tax, treaty benefits are generally claimed by you as the owner, not by the LLC.
  • A reduced withholding rate is claimed by giving the payer Form W-8BEN with a US or foreign taxpayer identification number. Some treaty positions also need Form 8833 attached to a US return. See the IRS page on claiming tax treaty benefits.
  • The treaty does not remove the LLC's information filings. Form 5472 is still due.

We do not quote treaty rates here. A US tax adviser should check the treaty text against your income types.

US filing checklist

  • EIN: send Form SS-4 to the IRS by fax or mail, writing "Foreign" in place of an SSN. See EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472 plus pro forma 1120: due April 15 for a calendar-year LLC with reportable transactions between you and the company. Missing it can cost $25,000 per form. See our Form 5472 guide.
  • BOI: not required. FinCEN's final rule effective August 14, 2026 permanently exempts companies formed in a US state. See BOI reporting in 2026.
  • ITIN: useful when you need to file a US personal return, claim a treaty position that calls for a US number, or a provider asks for one. See ITIN for non-residents.

Turkish tax reporting

Income tax in Turkey is administered by the Revenue Administration (Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı). If you are tax resident in Turkey, assume that your stake in a US company and what it pays you are relevant to your Turkish tax position, and that the treaty's rules on relief from double taxation may come into play. The exact treatment of a single-member US LLC under Turkish law, and how and when its profit is reported, are technical questions we do not summarise here. Work them out with a Turkish mali müşavir (certified accountant) and keep documents for every transfer between Turkey and the US.

Banking, Stripe and addresses

US business bank accounts are available to many non-resident LLC owners, but supported countries vary by bank and fintech, and change. Check the eligibility page of each provider and read US business accounts for non-residents.

  • On every application, your personal address is your real home address in Turkey, with matching proof.
  • Many banks and processors treat virtual mailbox (CMRA) addresses with caution or decline them as a business address. See what is a CMRA address.
  • Stripe accepts US-registered businesses owned from abroad, subject to verification of the company and representative. See Stripe for a non-US LLC owner.

Choosing a state

Turkish founders selling software, services or ecommerce often pick Wyoming for its $100 filing fee, $60 minimum annual report tax and lack of state income tax. Delaware is typical when raising US investment. See Wyoming vs Delaware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the US have a tax treaty with Turkey?

Yes. Turkey is on the IRS list of US income tax treaties, and the IRS publishes the 1996 treaty and its technical explanation.

Can I form a US LLC while living in Turkey?

Yes. There is no US citizenship or residency requirement for LLC members, and Turkey is not under comprehensive US sanctions.

Do I still file Form 5472 if a treaty applies?

Yes. Form 5472 is an information return for foreign-owned single-member LLCs with reportable transactions, and a treaty does not remove it.

Who handles Turkish tax on my US LLC income?

Turkey's Revenue Administration (Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı) administers income tax. Confirm with a Turkish certified accountant how to report the LLC.
