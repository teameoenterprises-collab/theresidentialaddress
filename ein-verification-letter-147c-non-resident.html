How to Get an EIN Verification Letter (147C) as a Non-Resident LLC Owner

🧾📠🔎

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: The IRS will not reissue the original EIN notice (CP575) you received when your LLC got its EIN. Instead, you call the IRS and ask for Letter 147C, "EIN Previously Assigned", which confirms the number in writing. The IRS can fax or mail it to the LLC's owner or to a representative authorized with Form 2848 or Form 8821. If you can sign in to an IRS Business Tax Account, you can also download a digital CP575, which the IRS says banks accept as the equivalent of a 147C.

Why you might need it

Banks, payment processors such as Stripe, marketplaces and some clients ask for an IRS document that shows your LLC's legal name next to its EIN. The usual document is the CP575 notice the IRS issued when it assigned the EIN. Non-resident founders who applied with Form SS-4 by fax often only kept a faxed copy, or never received the mailed notice at their foreign address. That is when a 147C helps.

CP575 vs Letter 147C

  • Original CP575 (the A to J series): issued once, when the EIN is assigned. The IRS states that it cannot be duplicated or recreated.
  • Letter 147C: a confirmation letter the IRS sends on request, at any time after the EIN was assigned.
  • Digital CP575: available only through the IRS Business Tax Account. The IRS says it is accepted by banks and other institutions as written confirmation of an EIN and is the equivalent of a 147C.

How to request a 147C from abroad

  1. Prepare the details. Have the LLC's exact legal name, the EIN if you know it, the business address the IRS has on file, and the responsible party's name ready. The assistor will ask identifying questions before releasing anything.
  2. Call the IRS. The Business & Specialty Tax Line is 800-829-4933, open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. local time, Monday to Friday. From outside the US, the IRS directs EIN callers to 267-941-1099. This is not a toll-free number, so international call charges apply.
  3. Ask for a Letter 147C. Say whether you want it faxed or mailed. Fax is much faster if you have a fax number you can receive on, including an online fax service. Mail goes to the address on file with the IRS, which can take weeks to reach you abroad.
  4. Check the letter. Confirm the legal name matches your state filing exactly before you send it to a bank.

Who is allowed to request it

The IRS gives EIN information only to a person authorized to receive it, such as an LLC's owner or a corporate officer. A formation agent, accountant or other third party needs a valid Form 2848 (power of attorney) or Form 8821 (tax information authorization) on file before the IRS will discuss the EIN or send a 147C to them. A third party designee named on your SS-4 had limited authority for the application itself, and IRS procedures say that authority does not extend to receiving mail.

If the IRS address on file is out of date

A mailed 147C goes to the address the IRS has for the LLC. If you have moved, or the address on your SS-4 was a service provider you no longer use, ask for the letter by fax, or update the address first with Form 8822-B. Do not give the IRS an address you do not actually use.

Common problems

  • Long hold times. Call early in the IRS working day and allow for more than one attempt.
  • Name mismatch. If the IRS has the LLC under a slightly different name than your articles, banks will notice. Ask the IRS how to correct the name on file.
  • Calling for someone else. Without Form 2848 or 8821, the IRS will not release the EIN to a helper, even with your permission on the call.
  • Lost EIN number. The IRS suggests checking the original notice, a previously filed return, or asking the bank or licensing agency where you used the EIN, before you call.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the IRS resend my original CP575 EIN notice?

No. The IRS says the original CP575A-J notice cannot be duplicated or recreated. Ask for Letter 147C instead, or download the digital CP575 from an IRS Business Tax Account if you can access one.

What number do I call for a 147C from outside the US?

The IRS directs EIN callers outside the US to 267-941-1099, which is not toll free. Inside the US, the Business & Specialty Tax Line is 800-829-4933, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. local time, Monday to Friday.

Can the IRS fax a 147C letter?

Yes. IRS procedures allow Letter 147C to be faxed or mailed to the taxpayer or an authorized representative.

Can my formation agent request the 147C for me?

Only if they have a valid Form 2848 or Form 8821 from you. A third party designee on Form SS-4 cannot receive mail on your behalf.

Do banks accept a 147C instead of the CP575?

Banks commonly accept Letter 147C as proof of an EIN. The IRS also says the digital CP575 from Business Tax Account is accepted by banks as written confirmation of an EIN.
