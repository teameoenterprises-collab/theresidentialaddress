How to Form a Wyoming LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🏔️📝💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Anyone can form a Wyoming LLC from abroad, with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file Articles of Organization with the Wyoming Secretary of State ($100, plus a small card fee online), appoint a Wyoming registered agent, then get an EIN from the IRS. Each year you pay a $60 minimum annual report license tax, renew the registered agent and file Form 5472 if you are the only owner.

Why non-residents choose Wyoming

  • Low cost: $100 to form and a $60 minimum each year.
  • No state income tax.
  • Owners' names are not required on the public formation filing.
  • Simple online filing that is usually approved quickly.

Delaware is the usual choice if you plan to raise US venture capital. Compare them in Wyoming vs Delaware.

Step by step

  1. Choose a name that is available on the Wyoming business search and ends in "LLC".
  2. Appoint a registered agent with a physical Wyoming address. This is required, and a P.O. box is not allowed.
  3. File the Articles of Organization online through WyoBiz ($100 plus a 2.4% card fee, minimum $1).
  4. Sign an operating agreement. It is not filed with the state, but banks often ask for it.
  5. Get an EIN with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See EIN without an SSN.
  6. Set up a US business address and bank account. See opening a US bank account as a non-resident.

What it costs

  • Articles of Organization: $100 (one time).
  • Annual report license tax: $60, or $0.0002 per dollar of Wyoming assets if higher. Due on the first day of your formation anniversary month.
  • Registered agent: typically $50 to $150 a year.
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

Yearly obligations

  • Wyoming annual report and license tax.
  • Registered agent renewal (missing it can lead to administrative dissolution).
  • Form 5472 with a pro forma 1120, due April 15, for single-member LLCs.
  • No FinCEN BOI report. See BOI in 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form a Wyoming LLC?

Yes. Wyoming has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a Wyoming registered agent with a physical address.

How much does a Wyoming LLC cost per year?

The state minimum is a $60 annual report license tax, plus registered agent fees of typically $50 to $150 a year.

Does a Wyoming LLC pay state income tax?

No. Wyoming has no state income tax. Federal filing obligations, such as Form 5472 for foreign-owned single-member LLCs, still apply.
