How Saudi Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇸🇦🏢🧾

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes. Saudi nationals and expatriates living in Saudi Arabia can form and own a US LLC with no US citizenship or residency. There is no income tax treaty between the US and Saudi Arabia in force. The LLC needs an EIN, files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year if you are its only owner, and is exempt from FinCEN BOI reporting. At home, zakat and tax matters are handled by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA).

Can you own a US LLC from Saudi Arabia?

Yes. No US state requires LLC members to be US citizens or to live in the US, and Saudi Arabia is not covered by a comprehensive US sanctions program on the OFAC sanctions programs page. The constraints you will meet are commercial: each bank, payment processor and marketplace decides which countries of residence and nationalities it supports. If you are an expatriate in Riyadh, Jeddah or Dammam, remember that your nationality may also matter for your own country's tax rules.

US tax treaty status

Saudi Arabia is not on the IRS list of US income tax treaties, so there is no treaty in force. As a result:

  • Reduced treaty withholding rates on US-source passive income are not available to you as a Saudi resident. IRS Publication 515 describes the general withholding rules for nonresidents.
  • Whether the LLC's trading profit is subject to US income tax depends on whether it is effectively connected with a US trade or business. Have a US tax adviser review how and where the business operates.

What the LLC files in the US

  • Employer Identification Number: requested with Form SS-4 by fax or mail, without an SSN. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472 with a pro forma 1120: required each year for a single-member LLC wholly owned by a foreign person when there are reportable transactions, such as capital you put in. Due April 15 for a calendar year; the penalty for not filing is $25,000. See Form 5472 explained.
  • No BOI report: FinCEN's final rule, effective August 14, 2026, permanently exempts US-formed companies. See BOI for non-resident LLCs in 2026.
  • ITIN if required: needed when you must file a personal US tax return, and requested by some providers. See getting an ITIN as a non-resident.

Zakat and tax in Saudi Arabia

In Saudi Arabia, zakat, income tax, VAT and customs are administered by ZATCA. Which of these is relevant to you, and whether your ownership of a US LLC or the profit it pays you belongs on a Saudi zakat or tax declaration, depends on your nationality, your residence and how your Saudi activities are structured. Saudi nationals and expatriates can be in quite different positions. We do not summarise those rules here because they are fact-specific. Confirm your treatment with a Saudi zakat and tax adviser before the LLC starts trading, and keep records of every transfer between your Saudi accounts and the US company.

Bank accounts and payments

US business accounts are open to many non-resident LLC owners, but providers set their own country and nationality lists and update them often. Read US business banking for non-residents and the provider's own eligibility page before you apply.

  • List the address where you actually live in Saudi Arabia as your personal residence, with documents that match.
  • Expect questions if the company's address is a virtual mailbox. Many banks and processors decline CMRA addresses. See what is a CMRA address.
  • For card payments, Stripe accepts US-registered businesses owned from abroad and verifies the LLC's EIN and legal name. See Stripe for non-US LLC owners.

Where to form

Wyoming is a frequent choice for Saudi-based founders with an online or services business because of low state fees and no state income tax. Delaware is common for companies that expect US investors. See Wyoming vs Delaware or our Wyoming guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Saudi resident own 100% of a US LLC?

Yes. A US LLC can have a single foreign owner, and there is no US citizenship or residency requirement.

Is there a US tax treaty with Saudi Arabia?

No. Saudi Arabia is not on the IRS list of US income tax treaties.

Does a US LLC owned from Saudi Arabia pay zakat?

It depends on your nationality, residence and how your activities are structured. ZATCA administers zakat and income tax, so confirm your position with a Saudi adviser.

What does a Saudi-owned single-member LLC file with the IRS each year?

Usually Form 5472 attached to a pro forma Form 1120, due April 15 for a calendar year, plus any other returns your facts require.
