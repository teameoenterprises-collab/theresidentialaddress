How to Form a Georgia LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🍑📄💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Anyone can form a Georgia LLC from abroad, with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file Articles of Organization with the Georgia Secretary of State's Corporations Division for $110 ($100 plus a $10 service charge), appoint a registered agent located in Georgia, then get an EIN from the IRS. Each year you file an annual registration for $60 between January 1 and April 1.

What Georgia charges

  • Articles of Organization: $100 filing fee plus a $10 service charge, $110 in total, under the fee schedule effective September 6, 2025.
  • Annual registration: $60 ($50 filing fee plus a $10 service charge).
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

The Secretary of State says filing fees are non-refundable. Expedited processing is available for an extra fee.

Step by step

  1. Check the name in the Georgia business search.
  2. Appoint a registered agent with a physical street address in Georgia.
  3. File the Articles of Organization online through eCorp or on paper with Form CD 030. The filing asks for the name and address of each organizer and of the registered agent.
  4. Sign an operating agreement. It is not filed, but banks often ask for it.
  5. Get an EIN with Form SS-4, then open a bank account.

The yearly annual registration

Every Georgia LLC files an annual registration between January 1 and April 1 of each year. The first one is due in the year after the year you form the LLC, so an LLC formed in 2026 files its first registration between January 1 and April 1, 2027. An LLC that does not file can be administratively dissolved.

Privacy: what is public

Georgia's Articles of Organization show the organizer and the registered agent. Listing managers is optional. The Secretary of State says Georgia law requires LLC annual registrations to list only the registered agent, along with the principal office mailing address and the person filing, so officers and members are not listed there. If you do not want your name on the articles, a formation service can act as organizer.

Federal steps for non-resident owners

These apply to every US LLC, whatever the state.

  • EIN: without an SSN or ITIN you apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472: a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year, due April 15, even with no income. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • BOI: LLCs formed in the US, including Georgia LLCs, are exempt from FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting under the final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.

Registered agent address vs business address

The registered agent address is where the state and courts send legal notices. It must be a physical street address in Georgia. It is not automatically your business or mailing address, and many agents do not forward everyday mail or let you use their address for banking.

Banks and payment providers usually ask for a business address and for each owner's real personal residential address, which for most non-residents is their home abroad. Many banks decline CMRA or virtual mailbox addresses as a business address, so check the bank's eligibility page before you apply. See what a CMRA address is.

Georgia vs Wyoming or Delaware

Georgia's costs are close to Wyoming's: $110 to form against $100, and $60 a year to the state in both. Georgia also keeps member names off the annual registration. The main differences are that Georgia has a state income tax and a fixed April 1 deadline for all LLCs. Georgia makes sense if you or your operations are in Georgia. For a remote online business, Wyoming avoids state income tax, and Delaware suits companies planning to raise venture capital. See Wyoming vs Delaware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form a Georgia LLC?

Yes. Georgia has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a registered agent located in Georgia.

How much does a Georgia LLC cost?

The Articles of Organization cost $110 including the service charge, and the annual registration costs $60 a year.

When is the Georgia annual registration due?

Between January 1 and April 1 each year, starting the year after you form the LLC.

Are owner names public for a Georgia LLC?

The articles show the organizer and registered agent, and listing managers is optional. The annual registration does not list members or officers.
