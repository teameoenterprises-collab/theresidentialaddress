How to Form a Virginia LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🏛️📄💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Anyone can form a Virginia LLC from abroad, with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file articles of organization with the Virginia State Corporation Commission (SCC) for $100, appoint a registered agent in Virginia, then get an EIN from the IRS. Each year the LLC pays a $50 annual registration fee by the last day of its anniversary month. Virginia LLCs do not file a separate annual report.

What Virginia charges

  • Articles of organization: $100, set by Virginia Code section 13.1-1005.
  • Annual registration fee: $50 a year, under section 13.1-1062.
  • Late penalty: $25 added to the fee if it is paid late, under section 13.1-1064.
  • Name reservation: $10, optional.
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

Step by step

  1. Check the name in the SCC business search. It must meet the naming rules in section 13.1-1012, which include an LLC designator.
  2. Appoint a registered agent with a registered office in Virginia (see below).
  3. File the articles of organization with the SCC. They state the LLC name, the registered office and agent, and the principal office address. The principal office does not need to be in Virginia, and members do not have to be named.
  4. Sign an operating agreement. It is not filed, but banks often ask for it.
  5. Get an EIN with Form SS-4, then open a bank account.

Who can be the registered agent

A Virginia LLC must continuously keep a registered office and a registered agent in Virginia. The agent can be an individual who lives in Virginia and holds one of the roles the statute lists, such as a member or manager of the LLC or a member of the Virginia State Bar, or a business entity authorized to do business in Virginia. An LLC cannot be its own registered agent. As a non-resident you will normally hire a commercial registered agent service.

The yearly registration fee and cancellation

The SCC assesses the $50 fee about two months before the LLC's anniversary month and sends a notice. Payment is due by the last day of the anniversary month. For example, an LLC formed in July pays by July 31 each year.

If the fee is not paid by the last day of the third month after the due date, the LLC's existence is automatically canceled. For a July LLC that means the end of October. Reinstatement is possible but adds cost and paperwork, so put the date in your calendar.

Federal steps for non-resident owners

These apply to every US LLC, whatever the state.

  • EIN: without an SSN or ITIN you apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472: a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year, even with no income. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • BOI: LLCs formed in the US, including Virginia LLCs, are exempt from FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting under the 2026 final rule. See BOI reporting in 2026.

Registered agent address vs business address

The registered office is where the state and courts send legal papers. It is not automatically your business or mailing address, and many agents do not forward everyday mail or let you use their address for banking.

Banks and payment providers usually ask for a business address and for each owner's real personal residential address, which for most non-residents is their home abroad. Many banks decline CMRA or virtual mailbox addresses as a business address, so check the bank's eligibility page before you apply. See what a CMRA address is.

Virginia vs Wyoming or Delaware

Virginia's yearly cost is low: $50 a year compared with Wyoming's $60 minimum and Delaware's $400. Unlike Wyoming, though, Virginia has a state income tax, so a Virginia LLC with Virginia business activity may have state tax filings. Virginia makes most sense if you, a co-founder or your operations are in Virginia, because an LLC doing business there would need to register there anyway. For a remote online business, compare Wyoming and read Wyoming vs Delaware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form a Virginia LLC?

Yes. Virginia has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC members. You need a registered agent and registered office in Virginia.

How much does a Virginia LLC cost?

The articles of organization cost $100 and the annual registration fee is $50 a year, with a $25 penalty if paid late.

When is the Virginia annual registration fee due?

By the last day of the month in which the LLC was formed, each year after formation. An LLC formed in July pays by July 31.

What happens if a Virginia LLC does not pay the annual fee?

If it is still unpaid by the last day of the third month after the due date, the LLC's existence is automatically canceled.
