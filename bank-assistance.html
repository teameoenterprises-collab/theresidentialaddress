Why US Banks Reject Non-US Founders

The Virtual Mailbox Trap

Mercury and Relay cross-reference your business address against the USPS CMRA (Commercial Mail Receiving Agency) database. If you use a standard Wyoming or EarthClassMail virtual box, you are auto-rejected.

Flagged VoIP Numbers

Registering with a Skype, OpenPhone, or Twilio number triggers high-risk fraud alerts in modern banking systems. VoIP numbers cannot receive short-code SMS from banks.
