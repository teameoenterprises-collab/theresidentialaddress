How to Form a Florida LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🌴📝💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: A non-US founder can form a Florida LLC with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file Articles of Organization with the Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz) for $125, which is the $100 filing fee plus the $25 registered agent designation fee, then get an EIN. Each year the LLC files an annual report between January 1 and May 1 for $138.75, and a foreign-owned single-member LLC also files Form 5472.

What a Florida LLC costs

  • Articles of Organization: $100 filing fee plus $25 to designate the registered agent, $125 in total.
  • Annual report: $138.75 a year ($50 report fee plus the $88.75 supplemental corporate fee), filed between January 1 and May 1.
  • Late annual report: a $400 late charge applies after May 1.
  • Registered agent: a private service charges an annual fee that varies by provider.
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

The first annual report is due between January 1 and May 1 of the year after the LLC is formed.

Step by step

  1. Check the name on Sunbiz. It must include "LLC", "L.L.C." or "Limited Liability Company".
  2. Appoint a Florida registered agent with a Florida street address.
  3. File the Articles of Organization online on Sunbiz.
  4. Sign an operating agreement. It is not filed, but banks often ask for it.
  5. Get an EIN from the IRS. Without an SSN or ITIN you apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  6. Set up a business address and bank account. See opening a US bank account as a non-resident.

Yearly obligations

  • Florida annual report and $138.75 fee by May 1.
  • Registered agent renewal.
  • Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year if the LLC is a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person, due April 15 for calendar-year filers. The IRS penalty for failing to file is $25,000.
  • No FinCEN BOI report. US-formed LLCs are exempt under FinCEN's final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.

Is your name public?

Usually yes. The Florida annual report must list the name and address of at least one person with authority to manage the company, along with the principal office and mailing addresses, and Sunbiz records are searchable by anyone. Plan on your name or a manager's name being visible.

Registered agent address vs business address

The registered agent address is where the state and courts send legal papers. It is not automatically your business address. Banks, payment processors and the IRS will also ask for a business or mailing address, and many banks decline CMRA or virtual mailbox addresses for that purpose. See what a CMRA address is, and Florida LLC address requirements. Always give a bank your real personal residential address, and check each bank's own eligibility page before applying.

When Florida makes sense

Florida is a practical choice if you will actually operate there, for example with a warehouse, staff, customers you meet in person or a lease in the state. If the LLC does business in Florida, forming there avoids registering a Wyoming or Delaware LLC as a foreign LLC in Florida as well. For a purely online business with no Florida activity, Wyoming costs less each year and keeps owner names off the state filing. See Wyoming LLC for non-residents. Florida has no personal state income tax, but an LLC that elects to be taxed as a corporation may owe Florida corporate income tax.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form a Florida LLC?

Yes. Florida has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a registered agent with a Florida street address.

How much is the Florida LLC annual report?

$138.75, filed between January 1 and May 1. A $400 late charge applies after May 1.

Are owner names public in Florida?

Usually. The annual report lists at least one person with authority to manage the LLC, and Sunbiz records are public.

Does a Florida LLC pay state income tax?

Florida has no personal income tax. An LLC taxed as a corporation may owe Florida corporate income tax.
