How Brazilian Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇧🇷🇺🇸💳

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes. A resident of Brazil can form and own a US LLC without US citizenship or residency. Unlike many countries, Brazil does not have a comprehensive income tax treaty with the US: it is not on the IRS list of treaty countries. Brazil's Law 14.754/2023 also sets rules on when Brazilian-resident individuals are taxed on profits of foreign entities they control, so plan the structure with a Brazilian tax adviser before trading through the LLC.

Can a Brazilian resident own a US LLC?

Yes. US states do not require LLC members to be US citizens, US residents or visa holders. From São Paulo, Rio or anywhere in Brazil you can file the state formation document online, appoint a registered agent in that state and request an EIN. Brazilian founders often use a US LLC to bill US and international clients in dollars or to sell through US platforms.

No US-Brazil income tax treaty

The IRS publishes a list of countries with which the US has an income tax treaty. Brazil is not on it. In practice that means you cannot rely on treaty rules to decide which country taxes LLC income first or to reduce US withholding. Each country applies its own law, and any relief for tax paid in the other country depends on domestic rules, which your adviser should check.

How Brazil looks at a US LLC

Law 14.754 of 12 December 2023 changed how Brazilian-resident individuals are taxed on investments and entities abroad. Its rules on controlled entities are the part most relevant to LLC owners:

  • An entity abroad counts as controlled when the individual holds more than 50% of its capital or has the power to direct its decisions or appoint its management. A single-member LLC you own usually meets this.
  • Profits from 2024 onward of controlled entities located in a low-tax jurisdiction or privileged tax regime, or that earn less than 60% of their total income as their own active income, are taxed on 31 December of each year whether or not they are distributed.
  • Profits of other controlled entities are taxed when they are made available to you, for example when paid or credited.
  • The law lets an individual choose to declare the controlled entity's assets, rights and obligations as if held directly. That choice is irrevocable while you hold the entity.

Which category your LLC falls into depends on where it is formed, how the US taxes it and what kind of income it earns. These are questions for a Brazilian adviser, especially because the US may tax a single-member LLC's income as yours while Brazil applies its own timing rules.

US filings for a Brazilian-owned LLC

  • EIN: Without an SSN or ITIN, apply on Form SS-4 by fax or mail, or by phone as an international applicant. See EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472: A single-member LLC owned by a non-US person generally files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 every year it has reportable transactions with its owner. The penalty for not filing is $25,000. See Form 5472 explained.
  • BOI: Not required for LLCs formed in a US state under FinCEN's final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.
  • ITIN: Not needed to form the LLC. You may need one if you have to file a US personal return. See ITIN for non-residents.

Banking and payments from Brazil

US banks and fintech providers decide for themselves which countries' residents they serve, and lists change. Some accept LLC owners resident in Brazil, others do not, and some require an in-person visit. Check each provider's eligibility page and read opening a US business bank account as a non-resident.

  • Applications usually ask for the LLC's formation documents, EIN letter, operating agreement and your passport.
  • Give your real home address in Brazil as your personal residential address.
  • Mailbox-store and virtual mailbox business addresses are often declined. See what is a CMRA address.

Stripe supports businesses in Brazil, so a Brazilian company can already accept payments locally. If you want the US LLC itself to receive US dollar payouts, it can open a US Stripe account. See Stripe for non-US LLC owners.

Which state Brazilian founders choose

Like most non-resident founders, Brazilians usually choose Wyoming or Delaware. Wyoming is popular for low yearly costs and no state income tax, while Delaware is preferred by many US investors. See Wyoming vs Delaware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Brazilian living in Brazil open a US LLC?

Yes. US states do not require LLC owners to be US citizens or residents. You need a registered agent in the state and an EIN from the IRS.

Is there a tax treaty between the US and Brazil?

No comprehensive income tax treaty. Brazil does not appear on the IRS list of countries with which the US has an income tax treaty.

How does Law 14.754/2023 affect a US LLC owner in Brazil?

It sets rules for foreign entities controlled by Brazilian-resident individuals. Some controlled entities' profits are taxed every 31 December, others when profits are made available, and there is an option to treat the entity's assets as held directly.

Does a Brazilian-owned LLC file Form 5472?

Generally yes, if it is a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person and has reportable transactions with its owner during the year.
