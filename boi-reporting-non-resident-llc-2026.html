BOI Reporting for Non-Resident Owned US LLCs in 2026

🏛️✅📋

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: No. An LLC formed in a US state does not have to file a FinCEN beneficial ownership information (BOI) report, even if all its owners live outside the US. FinCEN exempted US-formed companies in March 2025 and made that exemption permanent in a final rule effective August 14, 2026. Only companies formed outside the US and registered to do business in a state still report.

What changed

  • January 2024: the Corporate Transparency Act required most US and foreign companies to file BOI reports.
  • March 2025: FinCEN's interim final rule removed US-formed ("domestic") companies from reporting.
  • August 2026: FinCEN's final rule made the exemption permanent. US-formed companies have nothing to file, update or correct.

Who still files BOI

Only "foreign reporting companies": entities formed under another country's law that have registered to do business in a US state. If you formed your LLC in Wyoming, Delaware, Florida or any other state, it is a US company and is exempt, regardless of your citizenship or where you live.

What you still have to file

  • Form 5472 with a pro forma 1120 each year for a foreign-owned single-member LLC. See Form 5472 explained.
  • Your state annual report, for example Wyoming's report and $60 minimum license tax.
  • Registered agent renewal in your formation state.
  • Any US income tax returns that apply to your situation.

Watch out for BOI scams

Founders still receive letters and emails demanding BOI filings and fees. A US-formed LLC has no BOI obligation, and FinCEN never charges a filing fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my Wyoming LLC need to file BOI if I live outside the US?

No. US-formed LLCs are exempt from BOI reporting regardless of where the owners live.

When did BOI reporting end for US companies?

FinCEN exempted US-formed companies in March 2025 and made it permanent with a final rule effective August 14, 2026.

Is Form 5472 affected by the BOI exemption?

No. Form 5472 is an IRS filing and is still required annually for foreign-owned single-member LLCs with reportable transactions.
