How to Form a Texas LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🤠📝💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: A non-US founder can form a Texas LLC with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file a Certificate of Formation (Form 205) with the Texas Secretary of State for $300, name a Texas registered agent, then get an EIN. Each year by May 15 the LLC deals with Texas franchise tax: if annualized total revenue is at or below the no tax due threshold ($2.65 million for 2026 and 2027 reports), no tax is owed but a Public Information Report is still required.

What a Texas LLC costs

  • Certificate of Formation (Form 205): $300 (one time).
  • Franchise tax: $0 if annualized total revenue is at or below $2,650,000 for 2026 and 2027 reports. Above that, tax rates apply (0.75% for most businesses, 0.375% for retail or wholesale).
  • Public Information Report: required every year, with no filing fee.
  • Registered agent: a private service charges an annual fee that varies by provider.
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

Step by step

  1. Check the name with the Texas Secretary of State.
  2. Appoint a Texas registered agent with a Texas street address.
  3. File Form 205 online through SOSDirect or by mail. The form lists the name and address of each initial manager, or each member if there are no managers.
  4. Sign a company agreement. Texas calls the operating agreement a company agreement. It is not filed.
  5. Get an EIN from the IRS. Without an SSN or ITIN you apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  6. Set up a business address and bank account. See opening a US bank account as a non-resident.

Yearly obligations

  • Texas franchise tax report or Public Information Report by May 15. Since the 2024 report year, entities at or below the threshold no longer file a No Tax Due Report, but they still file the Public Information Report (Form 05-102).
  • Registered agent renewal.
  • Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year if the LLC is a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person, due April 15 for calendar-year filers. The IRS penalty for failing to file is $25,000.
  • No FinCEN BOI report. US-formed LLCs are exempt under FinCEN's final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.

Is your name public?

Yes. Form 205 lists the name and address of each initial governing person, and Secretary of State filings are public. Wyoming and Delaware formation filings do not require owner names.

Registered agent address vs business address

The registered agent address is where the state and courts send legal papers. It is not automatically your business address. Banks, payment processors and the IRS will also ask for a business or mailing address, and many banks decline CMRA or virtual mailbox addresses for that purpose. See what a CMRA address is, and Texas LLC address requirements. Always give a bank your real personal residential address, and check each bank's own eligibility page before applying.

When Texas makes sense

Texas fits founders who will actually operate in Texas, for example with a warehouse, staff or an office there. A Wyoming or Delaware LLC doing business in Texas would usually need to register in Texas as well. For an online business with no Texas presence, Wyoming has a lower formation fee ($100 vs $300) and keeps owner names off the formation filing. See Wyoming LLC for non-residents. Texas has no personal state income tax, but LLCs are subject to franchise tax.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form a Texas LLC?

Yes. Texas has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a registered agent with a Texas street address.

Does a small Texas LLC pay franchise tax?

Not if annualized total revenue is at or below the no tax due threshold, which is $2.65 million for 2026 and 2027 reports. It still files a Public Information Report.

When is the Texas franchise tax report due?

May 15 each year. If May 15 falls on a weekend or holiday, it is due the next business day.

Are owner names public for a Texas LLC?

Yes. The Certificate of Formation lists each initial manager, or each member if there are no managers.
