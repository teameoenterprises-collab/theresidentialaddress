How Long Does an ITIN Take in 2026? IRS Processing Times Explained

⏳🪪📬

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: The IRS says to allow 7 weeks to hear back about an ITIN application filed on Form W-7. It can take 9 to 11 weeks if you apply during tax season (January 15 to April 30) or if you apply from outside the US. When the ITIN is assigned, the IRS mails a CP565 notice with your number.

The official IRS timeframes

  • Normal processing: allow 7 weeks for the IRS to notify you of your application status.
  • Tax season, January 15 to April 30: 9 to 11 weeks.
  • Applying from overseas: 9 to 11 weeks.

The clock starts when the IRS receives a complete application, not when you post it. International mail time comes on top of these figures, so a founder mailing Form W-7 from abroad in February should plan for three months or more.

What usually adds delay

  • Missing tax reason. Form W-7 must be attached to a federal tax return or include proof that you meet one of the exceptions in the W-7 instructions. Without it, the application is suspended or rejected.
  • Document problems. The IRS needs original identity documents or copies certified by the issuing agency. A passport is the only document that proves both identity and foreign status on its own.
  • Requests for more information. If the IRS sends a CP566 notice, it needs more information, and your file waits until you reply.
  • Peak season. Applying between January 15 and April 30 moves you into the longer window.

The notices you may receive

  • CP565: your ITIN has been assigned. No further action is needed. Keep this letter safe.
  • CP566: the IRS needs more information to process the application.
  • CP567: the application was rejected and you must resubmit it.

How to check your ITIN status

If you have not heard from the IRS by the end of the processing window, call to ask about your application:

  • From outside the US: 267-941-1000 (not toll free).
  • From inside the US: 800-829-1040, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. local time.

When your passport comes back

If you mail original documents, the IRS returns them to the mailing address on line 2 of your Form W-7, and says to contact it if they have not arrived within 60 days. You can include a prepaid express or courier envelope to speed up the return. This is why many non-residents prefer a Certifying Acceptance Agent: a CAA can authenticate a passport so the original stays with you. Acceptance Agents and CAAs work both in the US and abroad.

How to plan around the wait

  • You do not need an ITIN to form an LLC or get an EIN. Apply for the EIN first so the business can start. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • If you need the ITIN for a tax return, apply outside the January to April peak when you can.
  • Make sure the mailing address on line 2 is one where you reliably receive post, because both the CP565 and your returned documents go there.

Keeping the ITIN active

An ITIN that has not been used on a US federal tax return for 3 consecutive tax years expires on December 31 of the third year. You renew it with a new Form W-7 before using it on a return again. If the ITIN only appears on information returns, the IRS says you do not need to renew it yet.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get an ITIN in 2026?

The IRS says to allow 7 weeks. It can take 9 to 11 weeks during tax season, January 15 to April 30, or if you apply from overseas.

How do I check my ITIN application status from abroad?

Call the IRS on 267-941-1000, which is not toll free. From inside the US the number is 800-829-1040.

What is the CP565 notice?

It is the IRS letter confirming that your ITIN has been assigned. No further action is needed.

When will the IRS return my passport?

The IRS mails original documents back to the address on line 2 of Form W-7 and says to contact it if they have not arrived within 60 days.

Does an ITIN expire?

Yes. If it is not used on a federal tax return for 3 consecutive tax years, it expires on December 31 of the third year and must be renewed with Form W-7.
