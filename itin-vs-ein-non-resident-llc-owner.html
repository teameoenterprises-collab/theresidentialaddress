ITIN vs EIN: Which One Does a Non-US LLC Owner Need?

🆔🏢🧾

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: An EIN is the tax number for your LLC. An ITIN is a personal tax number for you as an individual. A non-US founder's LLC needs an EIN, and you can get one without an SSN or ITIN. You only need an ITIN if you personally must file a US federal tax return or give a US tax number for yourself, for example to claim treaty benefits.

The two numbers side by side

  • EIN (Employer Identification Number): a 9-digit number the IRS assigns to businesses and other entities, such as LLCs, corporations and partnerships. It is applied for on Form SS-4 and is free.
  • ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number): a 9-digit number the IRS issues to individuals who need a US tax number but do not have, and are not eligible for, a Social Security number. It is applied for on Form W-7.

The IRS says not to use an EIN in place of an SSN or ITIN. The EIN belongs to the company and the ITIN belongs to the person, so they are not interchangeable.

Which one your LLC needs first

Get the EIN first. A US LLC with a foreign owner uses its EIN to open a bank account, set up Stripe or other payment processors, and file Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120. The IRS's Form SS-4 instructions cover a US disregarded entity that is wholly owned by a foreign person and needs an EIN to file Form 5472. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.

Getting an EIN without an ITIN

You do not need an ITIN to get an EIN. The Form SS-4 instructions say to enter "foreign" or N/A on line 7b if the responsible party does not have, and is not eligible for, an SSN or ITIN. What changes is how you apply:

  • Online: not available. The online application needs the responsible party to have an SSN, ITIN or EIN, and an applicant with no US residence or principal office cannot use it.
  • Phone: international applicants can call 267-941-1099 (not toll free), Monday to Friday, 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. Eastern time.
  • Fax: the IRS says an EIN by fax generally arrives within 4 business days.
  • Mail: about 4 weeks.

Step-by-step instructions are in how to get an EIN without an SSN.

When you also need an ITIN

The Form W-7 instructions say any individual who is not eligible for an SSN but must furnish a US taxpayer identification number or file a US federal tax return must apply for an ITIN. For a non-resident LLC owner, that typically means:

  • You must file a personal US return, such as Form 1040-NR, because your LLC has income effectively connected with a US trade or business.
  • You want to claim a tax treaty benefit that requires your own US tax number.
  • A bank, payment provider or marketplace asks for a personal US tax number, which some do.

An ITIN is for federal tax purposes only. It does not give you the right to work in the US or change your immigration status.

Applying for an ITIN from abroad

  • Attach a tax return: Form W-7 must generally be filed with a federal tax return, unless you meet one of the exceptions in the W-7 instructions.
  • Identity documents: a valid passport is the only document that proves both identity and foreign status on its own. Send originals or copies certified by the issuing agency, or use an IRS Certifying Acceptance Agent who can verify your passport.
  • Timing: the IRS says to allow 7 weeks, or 9 to 11 weeks in tax season or when you apply from abroad. See ITIN processing time.
  • Keep it active: an ITIN not used on a federal tax return for 3 consecutive tax years expires on December 31 of the third year.

For the full process, read how to get an ITIN as a non-resident.

Common mix-ups

  • Waiting for an ITIN before forming the LLC. Not needed. Form the LLC, get the EIN, and apply for the ITIN only when you have a reason to.
  • Putting the EIN on a personal form. Personal forms such as Form W-8BEN or Form 1040-NR use your own number, not the LLC's EIN. See W-8BEN or W-9 for a foreign-owned LLC.
  • Losing the EIN letter. The IRS can send an EIN verification letter, Letter 147C. See EIN verification letter 147C.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between an ITIN and an EIN?

An EIN is the IRS tax number for a business entity such as an LLC. An ITIN is a personal IRS tax number for an individual who is not eligible for a Social Security number.

Do I need an ITIN to get an EIN for my LLC?

No. On Form SS-4 you enter "foreign" or N/A on line 7b if you have no SSN or ITIN, then apply by fax, mail or the IRS international phone line.

Can I use my LLC's EIN instead of an ITIN?

No. The IRS says an EIN must not be used in place of an SSN or ITIN. Personal filings need your own number.

When does a non-resident LLC owner need an ITIN?

When you personally must file a US federal tax return, claim a treaty benefit that needs your own tax number, or a provider asks for a personal US tax number.

Does an ITIN expire?

Yes. If it is not used on a federal tax return for 3 consecutive tax years, it expires on December 31 of the third year and must be renewed before use.
