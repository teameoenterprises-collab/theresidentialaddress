How to Form a Michigan LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🚗📄💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Anyone can form a Michigan LLC from abroad, with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file articles of organization (Form 700) with the Michigan Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs (LARA) for $50 and name a resident agent at a Michigan street address. Each year the LLC files a $25 annual statement by February 15. Member and manager names are not required in the articles.

What Michigan charges

LARA's Corporations Division publishes its fees in its filing fee schedule.

  • Articles of organization (Form 700): $50, nonrefundable.
  • Annual statement: $25 a year, due February 15.
  • Restoring good standing after missed statements: $50, plus $25 for each missed annual statement.
  • Expedited service: optional. For formation documents, $50 for 24-hour and $100 for same-day service, up to $1,000 for one-hour service, on top of the filing fee.
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

Commercial resident agent fees are extra and set by each provider.

Step by step

  1. Check the name. It must include "Limited Liability Company", "L.L.C." or "L.C.".
  2. Choose a resident agent. The registered office must be a street address in Michigan. A business cannot act as its own resident agent.
  3. File Form 700 online through MiLogin for Business or on paper, with the $50 fee. The form asks for the LLC name, the resident agent's name, the registered office street address and the organizer's signature.
  4. Sign an operating agreement. It is kept by the company, not filed, and banks often ask for it.
  5. Get an EIN with Form SS-4, then open a bank account.

Form 700 is for ordinary businesses. Licensed professionals such as doctors, dentists and attorneys use Form 701 for a professional service LLC.

The resident agent rule

Michigan calls the registered agent a resident agent. The registered office is the agent's physical location in Michigan, where legal papers and state mail are delivered. Form 700 lets you add a separate mailing address for the registered office, which may be a PO box, but the registered office itself must be a street address.

Michigan does not record the LLC's business location in the articles, and the registered office does not need to be where you do business.

The annual statement

  • Due date: February 15 every year. Online filing opens on October 15 of the year before.
  • First year: an LLC formed after September 30 does not file on the February 15 right after formation.
  • Fee: $25. LARA's fee schedule lists a $50 late fee for professional LLCs only.

An LLC that stops filing loses good standing and can eventually be dissolved, along with the rights to its name. To fix it, the LLC files a certificate of restoration of good standing with every missed statement and the fees above. LARA says non-expedited restorations can take up to 10 business days.

Privacy: what is public

Michigan's articles of organization ask for the organizer's name and the resident agent's details, but not the names of members or managers. A manager-managed LLC only needs a statement in the articles that it will be managed by managers. If you use a formation service as organizer, your own name need not appear on the articles.

Federal steps for non-resident owners

These apply to every US LLC, whatever the state.

  • EIN: without an SSN or ITIN you apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472: a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year, due April 15, even with no income. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • BOI: LLCs formed in the US are exempt from FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting under the final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.

Michigan taxes to keep in mind

The $25 annual statement is a filing fee, not a tax. Michigan has state taxes, and some cities levy income taxes, that can apply to business activity in the state depending on where your sales, staff and property actually are. Check the Michigan Department of Treasury's guidance or ask a Michigan tax adviser before you rely on the LLC owing nothing there.

Resident agent address vs business address

The resident agent address is where the state and courts send legal notices. It is not automatically your business or mailing address, and many agents do not forward everyday mail or let you use their address for banking.

Banks and payment providers usually ask for a business address and for each owner's real personal residential address, which for most non-residents is their home abroad. Many banks decline CMRA or virtual mailbox addresses as a business address, so check the bank's eligibility page before you apply. See what a CMRA address is.

Michigan vs Wyoming or Delaware

Michigan's $50 formation fee and $25 annual statement are among the lower state costs, and member names stay off the articles. It fits best if you have customers, staff or inventory in Michigan. For a remote online business with no Michigan connection, compare Wyoming and Wyoming vs Delaware first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form a Michigan LLC?

Yes. Michigan has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a resident agent with a registered office at a Michigan street address.

How much does a Michigan LLC cost?

The articles of organization cost $50 and the annual statement is $25 a year. Resident agent fees are extra.

When is the Michigan annual statement due?

February 15 each year. An LLC formed after September 30 does not file on the February 15 that immediately follows.

What if my Michigan LLC misses annual statements?

It loses good standing and can eventually be dissolved. It can file a certificate of restoration of good standing for $50 plus $25 for each missed statement.

Are member names public for a Michigan LLC?

Not by default. Form 700 asks for the organizer and resident agent, not the members or managers.
