How Nepali Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇳🇵🇺🇸🧭

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: US law lets a Nepali resident own a US LLC, with no US citizenship or residency needed. Nepal is the hard part: outward investment by Nepali citizens has long been restricted under the Act Restricting Investment Abroad, 2021 BS (1964), with only limited routes opened since. There is also no US income tax treaty with Nepal. Get advice from a Nepali lawyer before forming or funding a US LLC.

What makes Nepal different

For most countries in South Asia, the question is how to route an investment abroad. For Nepal, the first question is whether you may make it at all. The Nepal Law Commission publishes the Act Restricting Investment Abroad, 2021 (1964), which applies to Nepali citizens. Recent reforms have opened some routes, mainly for IT businesses and for funds earned while living abroad, and further changes have been proposed. Because the position has been moving, check the current law and any Nepal Rastra Bank requirements with a qualified Nepali adviser before you act.

Nepal is not covered by any of the US Treasury's country-based sanctions programs, so the US side poses no special barrier.

No US-Nepal tax treaty

Nepal does not appear on the IRS list of US income tax treaties. In practice this means:

  • There are no treaty-reduced US withholding rates on US-source passive income such as dividends or interest paid to you as a Nepali resident.
  • Relief from double taxation depends on Nepali domestic law, not a treaty.
  • An ITIN is less often needed for treaty claims, but may still be needed to file a personal US return.

US filings for a Nepali-owned LLC

  • EIN: apply with Form SS-4 by phone, fax or mail. No SSN is needed. See getting an EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472 with a pro forma 1120: required every year for a foreign-owned single-member LLC, with a $25,000 penalty for not filing. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • BOI: US-created LLCs are exempt under FinCEN's final rule, effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.
  • ITIN: only when you must file a US personal return. See ITIN for non-residents.

Whether the LLC's income is taxable in the US depends on where and how the work is done. Get US tax advice if you have US staff, a US office or US-source income.

Tax and reporting in Nepal

Nepali residents should expect to report foreign income in Nepal. Given the investment restrictions, also ask your adviser how holding a foreign entity should be disclosed and how any profits should come home. Keep records of every transaction.

Banking and payments

Stripe does not list Nepal as a supported country. A US LLC with a US bank account can apply for a US Stripe account, subject to Stripe's review of the business and its owners. See Stripe for non-US LLC owners.

US banks and fintechs each decide which countries of residence they accept. Check the provider's eligibility page, then see opening a US business bank account as a non-resident. Your personal address on any application is your real home address in Nepal. The LLC's business address should not be a CMRA or virtual mailbox if the bank does not accept those. See what a CMRA address is.

State choice

If you do go ahead, Wyoming is a common low-cost choice, with a $100 filing fee and a $60 minimum annual report tax. See Wyoming LLC for non-residents.

Before you start

  1. Confirm with a Nepali lawyer that your investment is permitted and how it must be routed.
  2. Form the LLC and sign an operating agreement.
  3. Get the EIN and open a US business account.
  4. Calendar Form 5472 and state renewals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Nepali citizen own a US LLC?

US law allows it. Nepal restricts investment abroad by its citizens, so confirm with a Nepali lawyer that your investment is permitted before forming or funding the LLC.

Does the US have a tax treaty with Nepal?

No. Nepal is not on the IRS list of US income tax treaties.

Is Stripe available in Nepal?

Stripe does not list Nepal as a supported country. A US LLC can apply for a US Stripe account, subject to Stripe's review.

Does my LLC need to file a BOI report?

No. LLCs created in the US are exempt under FinCEN's final rule effective August 14, 2026.
