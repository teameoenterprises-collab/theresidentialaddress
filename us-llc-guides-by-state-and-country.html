US LLC Guides for Non-US Founders: Every State and Country Guide

🗺️🇺🇸🌍

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Non-US founders can form an LLC in any US state, but costs, annual fees and reporting rules differ a lot by state, and the right setup also depends on the country you live in. Use the state guides to compare filing fees and yearly costs, and the country guides for tax-treaty status, home-country rules, banking and payments.

US LLC guides by state

Each guide lists the official filing fee, the yearly fee or report and its due date, registered agent rules and what a non-resident owner needs to do after formation. Figures come from each state's Secretary of State or revenue department.

  • California: How non-US founders form a California LLC in 2026: $70 Form LLC-1, $20 Statement of Information, $800 annual tax, agent and EIN.
  • Colorado: How non-US founders form a Colorado LLC in 2026: $50 Articles of Organization, $25 periodic report, registered agent, EIN, Form 5472 and bank address tips.
  • Delaware: How non-US founders form a Delaware LLC in 2026: $110 filing fee, $400 annual tax due June 1, registered agent, EIN and yearly federal filings.
  • Florida: How non-US founders form a Florida LLC in 2026: $125 to file, $138.75 annual report due May 1, registered agent, EIN and federal filings.
  • Georgia: How non-US founders form a Georgia LLC in 2026: $110 Articles of Organization, $60 annual registration due April 1, registered agent, EIN and Form 5472.
  • Illinois: How non-US founders form an Illinois LLC in 2026: $150 Articles of Organization, $75 annual report, registered agent, managers listed, EIN and Form 5472.
  • Montana: How non-US founders form a Montana LLC in 2026: $35 Articles of Organization, April 15 annual report, registered agent, EIN, Form 5472 and address tips.
  • Nevada: How non-US founders form a Nevada LLC in 2026: $425 to start, $350 a year for the Annual List and business license, registered agent and EIN.
  • New Jersey: How non-US founders form a New Jersey LLC in 2026: $100 Certificate of Formation, $75 annual report, NJ registered agent, NJ-REG, EIN and Form 5472.
  • New Mexico: How non-US founders form a New Mexico LLC in 2026: $50 Articles of Organization, no annual report, registered agent, EIN and federal filings.
  • New York: How non-US founders form a New York LLC in 2026: $200 Articles, newspaper publication within 120 days, $50 certificate, $9 biennial statement.
  • North Carolina: How non-US founders form a North Carolina LLC in 2026: $125 Articles of Organization, $200 annual report due April 15, registered agent, EIN and Form 5472.
  • South Dakota: How non-US founders form a South Dakota LLC in 2026: $150 online filing, $55 annual report, no state income tax, registered agent, EIN and Form 5472.
  • Texas: How non-US founders form a Texas LLC in 2026: $300 Form 205, franchise tax and Public Information Report due May 15, registered agent and EIN.
  • Washington: How non-US founders form a Washington LLC in 2026: $180 Certificate of Formation, $70 annual report, governors listed, B&O tax, EIN and Form 5472.
  • Wyoming: How non-US founders form a Wyoming LLC in 2026: $100 state fee, $60 annual report, registered agent, EIN, bank account and yearly filings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which US state is cheapest for a non-resident LLC?

New Mexico has a low $50 filing fee and no annual report, and Wyoming charges $100 to form and a $60 minimum yearly fee. The cheapest choice depends on where your business actually operates.

Can I form a US LLC from any country?

Residents of most countries can form a US LLC. People in countries under comprehensive US sanctions face restrictions, so check OFAC rules first.

Do I need to live in the state where I form my LLC?

No. You need a registered agent with a physical address in that state, but owners can live anywhere.
