How to Form a Colorado LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🏔️📄💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Anyone can form a Colorado LLC from abroad, with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file Articles of Organization online with the Colorado Secretary of State for $50, appoint a registered agent with a Colorado street address, then get an EIN from the IRS. Each year you file a $25 periodic report, and a foreign-owned single-member LLC also files Form 5472.

What Colorado charges

  • Articles of Organization: $50, filed online. The Secretary of State does not accept paper for this filing.
  • Periodic Report: $25, filed online each year.
  • Late periodic report penalty: $50.
  • Changing the registered agent: $10 (Statement of Change).
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

Registered agent services are private businesses and set their own prices, so compare a few before you choose.

Step by step

  1. Check the name in the Colorado business database. It must include "LLC", "Limited Liability Company" or a similar ending.
  2. Appoint a registered agent with a physical street address in Colorado. The agent must consent to the appointment.
  3. File the Articles of Organization online. You give the principal office address, the registered agent, and the name and mailing address of the person forming the LLC, and you confirm the LLC has at least one member.
  4. Sign an operating agreement. Colorado does not ask you to file it, but banks often request it.
  5. Get an EIN with Form SS-4, then open a bank account.

The yearly periodic report

Each Colorado LLC has a periodic report month, shown on the entity's summary page in the Secretary of State's database. You can file from two months before that month to two months after it without a penalty. After that window the $50 late penalty applies automatically, and the Secretary of State says it cannot waive it.

Privacy: what is public

Colorado's Articles of Organization do not have a field for member names. They do show the registered agent, the principal office address and the name and mailing address of the person who forms the LLC, and anything you file becomes part of the public record. If you do not want your name on file, a formation service or agent can act as the person forming the LLC.

Federal steps for non-resident owners

These apply to every US LLC, whatever the state.

  • EIN: without an SSN or ITIN you apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472: a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year, due April 15, even with no income. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • BOI: LLCs formed in the US, including Colorado LLCs, are exempt from FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting under the final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.

Registered agent address vs business address

The registered agent address is where the state and courts send legal notices. It must be a physical street address in Colorado. It is not automatically your business or mailing address, and many agents do not forward everyday mail or let you use their address for banking.

Banks and payment providers usually ask for a business address and for each owner's real personal residential address, which for most non-residents is their home abroad. Many banks decline CMRA or virtual mailbox addresses as a business address, so check the bank's eligibility page before you apply. See what a CMRA address is.

Colorado vs Wyoming or Delaware

Colorado is one of the cheaper states to keep an LLC in good standing: $50 to form and $25 a year to the state. Unlike Wyoming, Colorado has a state income tax, which matters if the LLC has income from activity in Colorado. Colorado makes the most sense if you, a co-founder or your operations are in Colorado, since an LLC doing business there would need to register in Colorado anyway. For a remote online business with no US base, Wyoming is the more common pick, and Delaware suits companies planning to raise venture capital. Compare them in Wyoming LLC for non-residents and Wyoming vs Delaware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form a Colorado LLC?

Yes. Colorado has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a registered agent with a physical street address in Colorado.

How much does a Colorado LLC cost?

The state charges $50 for the Articles of Organization and $25 for each yearly periodic report. Registered agent fees are extra.

When is the Colorado periodic report due?

In your LLC's periodic report month, shown on its summary page. You can file up to two months before or two months after that month without a penalty.

Are owner names public for a Colorado LLC?

The Articles of Organization do not list members, but they do show the name and mailing address of the person who forms the LLC.
