How to Form a Montana LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🦬📄💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Anyone can form a Montana LLC from abroad, with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file Articles of Organization with the Montana Secretary of State for $35, appoint a registered agent with a Montana street address, then get an EIN from the IRS. Each year you file an annual report between January 1 and April 15. The Secretary of State currently waives the fee in that window and charges $35 after April 15.

What Montana charges

  • Articles of Organization: $35, plus $50 for each series member if you form a series LLC.
  • Annual report filed by April 15: the fee is currently waived.
  • Annual report filed after April 15: $35.
  • Changing the registered agent or office: no fee.
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

The fee waiver is a decision of the Secretary of State's office, not a permanent rule, so check the fee page before you file each year.

Step by step

  1. Check the name in the Montana business search.
  2. Appoint a registered agent with a physical street address in Montana.
  3. File the Articles of Organization online through the Secretary of State's business portal.
  4. Sign an operating agreement. It is kept by the company, not filed, and banks often ask for it.
  5. Get an EIN with Form SS-4, then open a bank account.

The yearly annual report

Montana law sets one window for every LLC. The first annual report is due between January 1 and April 15 of the year after the year you form the LLC, and every report after that is due in the same window. An LLC formed in November 2026, for example, files its first report between January 1 and April 15, 2027.

Privacy: what is public

Montana is less private than Wyoming. Under Montana's LLC statute, the articles list the names and business mailing addresses of the initial members, or of the managers if the LLC is manager-managed, and the annual report repeats this information. These filings are public, so expect your name to be visible in the state's records.

Federal steps for non-resident owners

These apply to every US LLC, whatever the state.

  • EIN: without an SSN or ITIN you apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472: a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year, due April 15, even with no income. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • BOI: LLCs formed in the US, including Montana LLCs, are exempt from FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting under the final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.

Registered agent address vs business address

The registered agent address is where the state and courts send legal notices. It must be a physical street address in Montana. It is not automatically your business or mailing address, and many agents do not forward everyday mail or let you use their address for banking.

Banks and payment providers usually ask for a business address and for each owner's real personal residential address, which for most non-residents is their home abroad. Many banks decline CMRA or virtual mailbox addresses as a business address, so check the bank's eligibility page before you apply. See what a CMRA address is.

Montana vs Wyoming or Delaware

Montana is one of the cheapest states to form an LLC, at $35, and the annual report fee has recently been waived when filed on time. The trade-off is privacy: member or manager names appear on public filings, while Wyoming does not require owner names on the formation filing. Montana also has a state income tax, which can matter if the LLC earns income from Montana activity. Montana fits best if you or your operations are actually in Montana. For a remote business, compare Wyoming and Wyoming vs Delaware first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form a Montana LLC?

Yes. Montana has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a registered agent with a physical street address in Montana.

How much does a Montana LLC cost to form?

The Articles of Organization cost $35. Registered agent fees are extra.

When is the Montana annual report due?

Between January 1 and April 15 each year, starting the year after you form the LLC. The fee is currently waived in that window and is $35 after April 15.

Are owner names public for a Montana LLC?

Usually yes. The articles and annual report list the names and business mailing addresses of the members or managers.
