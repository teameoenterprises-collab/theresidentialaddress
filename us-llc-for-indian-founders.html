US LLC for Indian Founders: What You Need to Know

🇮🇳🇺🇸🏢

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes, an Indian resident can own a US LLC without living in the US. You form it in a state such as Wyoming or Delaware, get an EIN from the IRS, and open a US bank account. Two sets of rules apply: on the Indian side, money you send to the company falls under RBI foreign exchange rules, including the Liberalised Remittance Scheme limit of USD 250,000 per person per financial year; on the US side, a single-member LLC owned by a foreign person must file Form 5472 every year.

Step 1: Check whether Indian overseas investment rules apply to you

India's foreign exchange rules (FEMA) treat an investment in a foreign company as overseas investment. Stripe's guide for Indian founders says these extra steps apply to residents of India for FEMA purposes, which broadly means you stayed in India more than 182 days in the previous financial year and have not moved abroad permanently.

  • LRS limit. Each Indian resident can remit up to USD 250,000 abroad per financial year under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme. Company formation fees paid by card are normally made under LRS.
  • Your AD bank. Overseas investment is reported through an Authorised Dealer (AD) bank, which issues a unique identification number (UIN) for the investment and handles the reporting forms.
  • Yearly reporting. Stripe's guide lists an Annual Performance Report (APR) filed with the AD bank by 31 December each year, and, where an Indian entity holds the investment, the RBI's Form FLA by 15 July each year.

The exact route depends on how much you invest, whether you plan to raise venture capital and whether the shares are held by you or by an Indian entity. Stripe's guide, for example, routes founders who want venture funding through an Indian LLP that owns a US C corporation. Speak to your AD bank and an Indian chartered accountant before you send money.

Step 2: Choose a state and form the LLC

You do not need a US visa, SSN or US address of your own to form an LLC. You do need a registered agent with a physical address in the state you choose. Many founders compare Wyoming and Delaware first; see Wyoming vs Delaware LLC and Wyoming LLC for non-residents. Check the state's Secretary of State website for the current filing fee and annual report fee.

Step 3: Get an EIN

Without an SSN or ITIN you apply for the LLC's Employer Identification Number on Form SS-4 by fax or mail, or by phone from abroad. See how to get an EIN without an SSN. Your PAN does not replace the EIN; the EIN is the company's US tax number.

Step 4: Open a US bank account and payment processor

Most Indian founders open accounts with online banks and fintechs that accept US-formed companies with owners abroad. Expect to provide formation documents, the EIN letter, your passport and your real home address in India. Banks also ask about your US operations or plans. See opening a US business bank account as a non-resident and Stripe for non-US LLC owners.

Step 5: Plan for US and Indian tax filings

  • Form 5472. A US single-member LLC wholly owned by a foreign person files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year it has reportable transactions with its owner, such as money put in or taken out. The penalty for not filing is $25,000. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • The US-India tax treaty. The United States has an income tax treaty with India, which helps avoid the same income being taxed twice. Whether the LLC owes US income tax depends on whether it has income effectively connected with a US trade or business.
  • Indian tax. Indian residents are generally taxed in India on their worldwide income, so LLC profits are usually reported in your Indian return as well. Foreign assets also have to be disclosed. Your chartered accountant should handle this.
  • BOI. US-formed LLCs no longer file FinCEN beneficial ownership reports. See BOI reporting for non-resident LLCs.

Do you need an ITIN?

Not to form the LLC or get its EIN. An ITIN is a personal US tax number, needed when you must file a US personal tax return and sometimes requested by payment processors. See how to get an ITIN as a non-resident.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an Indian resident own a US LLC?

Yes. There is no US citizenship or residency requirement to own an LLC. Indian residents must also follow RBI foreign exchange rules when sending money to the company.

How much money can I send to my US LLC from India?

Under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme each resident individual can remit up to USD 250,000 per financial year. Investment in a foreign company is also subject to overseas investment rules, reported through your AD bank.

Does an Indian-owned US LLC need to file Form 5472?

A single-member US LLC wholly owned by a foreign person generally files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 every year it has reportable transactions with its owner. The penalty for not filing is $25,000.

Is there a tax treaty between the US and India?

Yes. The IRS lists an income tax treaty with India. It helps prevent double taxation, but you still report income in India as an Indian tax resident.

Do I need an ITIN to form a US LLC from India?

No. You can form the LLC and get an EIN without an ITIN. An ITIN is for personal US tax filings and is sometimes requested by payment processors.
