How to Form a Washington LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🌲📄💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Anyone can form a Washington LLC from abroad, with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file a Certificate of Formation with the Washington Secretary of State for $180, appoint a registered agent with a Washington street address, then get an EIN from the IRS. Each year you file a $70 annual report by the end of your formation anniversary month.

What Washington charges

  • Certificate of Formation: $180.
  • Initial report: free if filed with the Certificate of Formation, or $10 if filed separately within the first 120 days.
  • Annual report: $70.
  • Delinquency fee: $25 if the LLC's status is listed as delinquent.
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

Step by step

  1. Check the name in the Washington business search.
  2. Appoint a registered agent with a physical street address in Washington.
  3. File the Certificate of Formation online with the Secretary of State, and file the initial report at the same time to avoid the separate $10 fee.
  4. Sign an operating agreement. It is not filed, but banks often ask for it.
  5. Get an EIN with Form SS-4, then open a bank account.

The yearly annual report

The annual report is due by the LLC's expiration date, which the Secretary of State defines as the last day of the month in which the business was first formed. You can file up to 180 days early. An LLC formed in March is due by March 31 each year.

Privacy: what is public

Washington reports must list at least one governor. For an LLC, the Secretary of State treats members and managers as governors, and a business cannot be its own governor. These reports are public, so expect at least one owner or manager name to appear in the state's records.

Washington taxes

Washington has no income tax, but the Department of Revenue says most businesses in the state are subject to the business and occupation (B&O) tax, a tax on gross receipts with no deduction for costs. Rates depend on the type of activity. If your LLC does business in Washington, register with the Department of Revenue and check which classification applies.

Federal steps for non-resident owners

These apply to every US LLC, whatever the state.

  • EIN: without an SSN or ITIN you apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472: a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year, due April 15, even with no income. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • BOI: LLCs formed in the US, including Washington LLCs, are exempt from FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting under the final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.

Registered agent address vs business address

The registered agent address is where the state and courts send legal notices. It must be a physical street address in Washington. It is not automatically your business or mailing address, and many agents do not forward everyday mail or let you use their address for banking.

Banks and payment providers usually ask for a business address and for each owner's real personal residential address, which for most non-residents is their home abroad. Many banks decline CMRA or virtual mailbox addresses as a business address, so check the bank's eligibility page before you apply. See what a CMRA address is.

Washington vs Wyoming or Delaware

Washington costs more than Wyoming to form ($180 vs $100) and to keep ($70 vs a $60 minimum), it publishes at least one governor's name, and its B&O tax applies to gross receipts from Washington business. It makes sense if you, your team or your operations are in Washington, because an LLC doing business there would need to register there anyway. For a remote online business, Wyoming is usually simpler. See also Wyoming vs Delaware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form a Washington LLC?

Yes. Washington has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a registered agent with a physical street address in Washington.

How much does a Washington LLC cost?

The Certificate of Formation is $180 and the annual report is $70. The initial report is free if filed with the formation.

When is the Washington annual report due?

By the last day of the month in which the LLC was formed, each year. You can file up to 180 days early.

Does a Washington LLC pay state income tax?

Washington has no income tax, but most businesses doing business there owe the B&O tax on gross receipts.
