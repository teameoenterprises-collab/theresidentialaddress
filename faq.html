Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US resident form a US LLC?

Yes. No US state requires LLC owners to be US citizens or residents. You need a registered agent with a physical address in the state where you form the LLC.

Which state is best for a non-resident's LLC?

Wyoming suits most non-resident founders: $100 to form, a $60 minimum annual report and no state income tax. Delaware is usually preferred if you plan to raise US venture capital.

Can I get an EIN without an SSN?

Yes. File Form SS-4 by fax (about 4 business days) or mail (about 4 weeks), writing “Foreign” on line 7b. The IRS online application requires an SSN or ITIN.

Do I need an ITIN to open a US business bank account?

Usually not. Most online business accounts need the company's EIN and the owners' passports. An ITIN helps with personal banking and building US credit.

How long does an ITIN take?

Usually 7 to 11 weeks after the IRS receives a complete Form W-7, and longer in tax season or when applying from abroad.

Why do banks reject virtual mailbox addresses?

Virtual mailboxes are registered with the USPS as Commercial Mail Receiving Agencies (CMRAs). Banks check this automatically and many decline CMRA addresses as a company's address.

What residential address should I give my bank?

The address where you actually live. For most non-resident founders this is their home address abroad, which online banks that accept non-residents expect.

Does my US LLC need to file a BOI report?

No. FinCEN permanently exempted US-formed companies from BOI reporting, effective August 14, 2026, regardless of where the owners live.

Does a foreign-owned single-member LLC have to file Form 5472?

Generally yes, every year it has a reportable transaction with its owner, such as funding the LLC. The penalty for not filing is $25,000 per form.
