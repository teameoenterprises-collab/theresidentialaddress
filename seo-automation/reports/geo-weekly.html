How these numbers are measured

Rankings come from a daily web search for each tracked phrase, recording whether theresidentialaddress.com appears in the top results and who ranks first. The search runs from the US, so it is a sample, not every country's results. Indexing and impressions come from Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, which only the owner can open; screenshots shared with Claude are added to the next report. Page counts and work done come from the site's commit history.
