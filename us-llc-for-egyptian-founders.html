How Egyptian Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇪🇬🏛️💼

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes. People living in Egypt can form a US LLC without a US visa, Green Card or US address of their own. Egypt has an income tax treaty with the United States that appears on the IRS treaty list. The LLC still has its own US obligations: an EIN, an annual Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 for a single foreign owner, and no FinCEN BOI report since US-formed companies are exempt.

Eligibility and sanctions

US LLC law has no nationality or residency test for members, so an Egyptian resident can be the sole owner and manager. Egypt is not covered by a comprehensive US sanctions program on the OFAC sanctions programs list. Banks and processors still run their own checks on every owner. For the general rules, see can you open a US LLC without living in America.

The US-Egypt income tax treaty

Egypt is listed on the IRS list of US income tax treaties, and the IRS publishes the treaty text and technical explanation, both dated 1980. A treaty in force does not mean your LLC is automatically tax free. Points to keep in mind:

  • A single-member LLC is normally disregarded for US income tax, so treaty questions are usually answered at the level of you, the owner, not the LLC.
  • To claim a reduced withholding rate you generally give the payer Form W-8BEN with a US or foreign taxpayer identification number, and some treaty positions must be disclosed on Form 8833 with a US return. The IRS sets this out on claiming tax treaty benefits.
  • Whether you owe US tax at all depends mainly on whether the business has income effectively connected with a US trade or business.

Treaty analysis is specialist work. Have a US tax adviser read the treaty against your facts before relying on it.

Filings in the US

  • EIN through Form SS-4, sent by fax or mail, with no SSN needed. See EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472 attached to a pro forma Form 1120 for each year with reportable transactions between you and the LLC. Due April 15 for a calendar year, with a $25,000 penalty for failure to file. See our Form 5472 guide.
  • No BOI filing. FinCEN's final rule effective August 14, 2026 permanently removed US-formed companies from beneficial ownership reporting. See BOI in 2026.
  • ITIN when you personally must file a US return, claim a treaty position that requires a US number, or a provider insists on one. See ITIN for non-residents.

Reporting in Egypt

As an Egyptian tax resident you should expect your interest in a foreign company, and the income it pays you, to be relevant to your Egyptian tax position. The Egyptian Tax Authority administers income tax, and the treaty with the US contains rules for relieving double taxation that an adviser can apply to your case. We have not summarised Egyptian residence or foreign income rules here because they depend on your circumstances. Speak to an Egyptian accountant about how to report the LLC, and keep bank records of every transfer between Egypt and the US account.

Opening accounts and taking payments

Non-resident owners can usually open a US business account for their LLC, but whether a given bank or fintech accepts residents of Egypt is set by that provider and can change. Check its eligibility page first and read US business bank accounts for non-residents.

  • Give the bank your actual home address in Egypt as your residential address, supported by documents in your name.
  • For the company's address, note that many banks and processors decline commercial mail receiving agency (CMRA) addresses. Read what is a CMRA address.
  • Stripe accepts US-registered businesses run from abroad and verifies the company, its EIN and the representative. See Stripe for non-US LLC owners.

Which state

For a small business run from Cairo or Alexandria, Wyoming is frequently chosen for its low fees and lack of state income tax. Delaware is the standard for companies expecting US venture capital. See Wyoming vs Delaware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a US tax treaty with Egypt?

Yes. Egypt appears on the IRS list of US income tax treaties, and the IRS publishes the treaty and its technical explanation, both dated 1980.

Does the treaty mean my US LLC pays no US tax?

Not automatically. A single-member LLC is usually disregarded, so treaty benefits are claimed by the owner with forms such as W-8BEN or Form 8833, and whether US tax applies depends on your facts.

Can an Egyptian resident open a US business bank account for the LLC?

Often yes, but each bank or fintech decides which countries of residence it supports. Check the provider's current eligibility page before applying.

Do I file a BOI report for my US LLC?

No. Companies formed in a US state are exempt from FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting under a final rule effective August 14, 2026.
