How Vietnamese Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇻🇳🏢🌏

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes, US law lets a founder living in Vietnam form and own a US LLC with no US citizenship or residency, and there is no US-Vietnam income tax treaty in force. The US side is routine: an EIN, an annual Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 for a sole foreign owner, and no FinCEN BOI report. The Vietnamese side needs more planning, because Vietnam's Law on Investment treats setting up a company abroad as outward investment, with registration and capital account rules.

Can Vietnamese residents own a US LLC?

From the US point of view, yes. State LLC laws do not limit who can be a member, and Vietnam is not covered by a comprehensive US sanctions program on the OFAC sanctions programs list. The more important question for founders in Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi is what Vietnamese law requires when money leaves Vietnam to fund a foreign company. That is covered below.

No tax treaty in force

Vietnam is not on the IRS list of US income tax treaties, so no treaty is in force. That means:

  • No treaty-reduced US withholding on dividends, interest or royalties paid to you personally.
  • US income tax on the LLC's profit depends on whether the income is effectively connected with a US trade or business. The IRS explains this on its effectively connected income page. A US tax adviser can tell you where your business falls.

Vietnam's outward investment rules

Vietnam's Law on Investment (No. 61/2020/QH14), effective January 1, 2021, lists "establishment of a business organization in accordance with the law of the host country" as a form of outward investment. The law sets out several requirements that can apply:

  • Outward investment registration certificate. Article 60 lists conditions for the certificate, including a commitment to arrange foreign currency and a tax authority certification that the investor has met tax obligations.
  • Capital account. Article 65 requires an outward investment capital account at an authorized credit institution in Vietnam, and money moving to and from Vietnam for the investment goes through that account.
  • Transferring capital. Under Article 66, capital is generally transferred abroad only after the registration certificate is granted.
  • Repatriating profit. Article 68 requires profit not reinvested under the law to be repatriated within 6 months of the host country tax declaration, with a possible extension.

The law names the Ministry of Planning and Investment as the registration authority, and its implementing decree and later changes affect how this works for individuals and small amounts. Confirm the current procedure with a Vietnamese lawyer before you fund the LLC, and ask a Vietnamese tax adviser how the LLC's income should be reported on your personal return.

What the LLC files with US authorities

  1. EIN via Form SS-4 by fax or mail, without an SSN. See EIN without an SSN.
  2. Form 5472 with a pro forma 1120 each year with reportable transactions, due April 15 for a calendar year, with a $25,000 penalty for not filing. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  3. No BOI report. US-formed companies are permanently exempt under FinCEN's final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI in 2026.
  4. ITIN only if you need a personal US tax number. See ITIN for non-residents.

Banking and payments

US business accounts for non-resident LLC owners exist, but each provider decides which countries it supports and changes its list over time. Read US business banking for non-residents and check the provider's eligibility page.

  • Use the address where you live in Vietnam as your personal address, with proof in your name.
  • Virtual mailbox (CMRA) addresses are often flagged or refused as a company address. See what is a CMRA address.
  • Stripe supports US-registered companies with foreign owners after verifying the company and representative. See Stripe for non-US LLC owners.

Which state

Wyoming is a common choice for Vietnamese founders running ecommerce, software or service businesses, thanks to low fees and no state income tax. Delaware suits companies raising from US investors. See Wyoming LLC for non-residents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a US-Vietnam tax treaty in force?

No. Vietnam is not on the IRS list of US income tax treaties in force.

Do I need Vietnamese approval to set up a US LLC?

Possibly. Vietnam's Law on Investment treats establishing a business abroad as outward investment, with an outward investment registration certificate and a dedicated capital account. Confirm the current procedure with a Vietnamese lawyer.

Can I get an EIN from Vietnam without an SSN?

Yes. Send Form SS-4 to the IRS by fax or mail. No SSN or ITIN is required.

Does Vietnam require profit from a foreign company to be brought home?

The Law on Investment generally requires profit from outward investment that is not reinvested to be repatriated within 6 months of the host country tax declaration, with a possible extension.
