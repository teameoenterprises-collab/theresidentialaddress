How Indonesian Founders Can Form and Run a US LLC (2026 Guide)

🇮🇩🇺🇸🚀

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Yes. An Indonesian resident can form and own a US LLC with no US citizenship or residency. Indonesian resident taxpayers are generally taxed on income from inside and outside Indonesia, so the LLC's profits need to be part of your Indonesian tax planning. The US has an income tax treaty with Indonesia. On the US side, plan for an EIN, a yearly Form 5472 and a US business bank account.

Why Indonesian founders use a US LLC

Indonesian startups, agencies and creators selling software, design and digital products to US buyers often want a US contracting entity and US payment rails. Stripe lists Indonesia as a preview market, so access in Indonesia itself is limited. Indonesia is not covered by any of the US Treasury's country-based sanctions programs.

Indonesian tax on foreign income

Under Indonesia's Income Tax Law (Law No. 36 of 2008, as amended), as explained by the Directorate General of Taxes:

  • Resident individual taxpayers are taxed on income received from Indonesia and from abroad.
  • Tax paid abroad can be credited against Indonesian tax on the same income in the same year, up to the Indonesian tax on that income.
  • Since the Job Creation Law, dividends from abroad received by resident taxpayers can be excluded from income tax if they are reinvested in Indonesia within a set period and other conditions are met.

Whether money you take out of a single-member US LLC counts as a dividend for these rules, and how to report your holding in your annual return (SPT Tahunan), depends on your facts. Confirm with an Indonesian tax consultant before you rely on any exemption.

Is there a US-Indonesia tax treaty?

Yes. The IRS lists an income tax treaty between the United States and Indonesia. It can affect withholding on some US-source income and how double tax is relieved. You will usually need a US taxpayer identification number to claim it.

US filings

  • EIN: Form SS-4, no SSN needed. See EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472 with a pro forma 1120: filed every year by a foreign-owned single-member LLC, with a $25,000 penalty for not filing. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • BOI: US-created LLCs are exempt from FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting under the final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI in 2026.
  • ITIN: if you need to file a personal US return or claim treaty benefits. See ITIN for non-residents.

Moving money and reporting at home

Expect your Indonesian bank to ask about the purpose of large outgoing transfers and for supporting documents. Ask what it requires before you capitalize the LLC, and keep records of every transfer and distribution for your SPT.

Banking and Stripe

A US LLC with a US business bank account can apply for a US Stripe account. Stripe reviews the business and its owners and may ask for more documents, so approval is not automatic. See Stripe for non-US LLC owners.

US banks and fintechs that serve non-resident owners each publish eligibility rules that change over time. Check the provider's current page, then read opening a US business bank account as a non-resident. Use your real home address in Indonesia as your personal address. For the LLC's business address, banks often decline CMRA and virtual mailbox addresses. See what a CMRA address is.

Choosing a state

Wyoming is a popular low-cost choice, with a $100 filing fee and a $60 minimum annual report tax. Indonesian startups planning to raise from US investors often look at Delaware. See Wyoming LLC for non-residents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an Indonesian resident own a US LLC?

Yes. US states do not require LLC owners to be US citizens or residents.

Is my US LLC income taxed in Indonesia?

Resident taxpayers are generally taxed on income from Indonesia and abroad, with a credit for foreign tax paid. Some reinvested foreign dividends can be exempt. Check with an Indonesian tax consultant.

Does the US have a tax treaty with Indonesia?

Yes. The IRS lists a US income tax treaty with Indonesia.

Is Stripe available in Indonesia?

Stripe lists Indonesia as a preview market. Many founders apply through a US LLC with a US bank account instead, subject to Stripe's review.
