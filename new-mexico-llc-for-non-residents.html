How to Form a New Mexico LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🌵📝💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: A non-US founder can form a New Mexico LLC with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file Articles of Organization online with the New Mexico Secretary of State for $50 and appoint a New Mexico registered agent. New Mexico does not require LLCs to file an annual or periodic report, which makes it one of the cheapest states to keep an LLC in. Federal filings such as Form 5472 still apply.

What a New Mexico LLC costs

  • Articles of Organization: $50 (one time). The Secretary of State now takes business filings online only.
  • Annual report: none required for LLCs.
  • Registered agent: a private service charges an annual fee that varies by provider.
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

Step by step

  1. Check the name on the New Mexico business search.
  2. Appoint a New Mexico registered agent with a physical New Mexico address.
  3. File the Articles of Organization through the Secretary of State's online business portal.
  4. Sign an operating agreement. It is not filed, but banks often ask for it.
  5. Get an EIN from the IRS. Without an SSN or ITIN you apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  6. Set up a business address and bank account. See opening a US bank account as a non-resident.

Yearly obligations

  • No New Mexico annual report.
  • Registered agent renewal. Keep the agent current, because the state has no yearly filing that would prompt you to update it.
  • Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year if the LLC is a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person, due April 15 for calendar-year filers. The IRS penalty for failing to file is $25,000.
  • No FinCEN BOI report. US-formed LLCs are exempt under FinCEN's final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.

Is your name public?

New Mexico is often described as a private state because there is no annual report listing owners. The current online Articles of Organization form does include a member and manager information section, so check what your filing will show on the public record before you submit it.

Registered agent address vs business address

The registered agent address is where the state and courts send legal papers. It is not automatically your business address. Banks, payment processors and the IRS will also ask for a business or mailing address, and many banks decline CMRA or virtual mailbox addresses for that purpose. See what a CMRA address is, and registered agent vs business address. Always give a bank your real personal residential address, and check each bank's own eligibility page before applying.

When New Mexico makes sense

New Mexico suits founders who want the lowest ongoing state cost and fewer filings. The tradeoff is that New Mexico is less familiar to some banks and investors than Wyoming or Delaware, and an LLC that does business in New Mexico may owe the state's gross receipts tax and income tax. Wyoming costs a little more ($60 a year minimum) and has no state income tax. See Wyoming LLC for non-residents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form a New Mexico LLC?

Yes. New Mexico has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a registered agent with a New Mexico address.

Does a New Mexico LLC file an annual report?

No. New Mexico does not require LLCs to file an annual or periodic report.

How much does it cost to form a New Mexico LLC?

The state fee for the Articles of Organization is $50. Registered agent fees are extra.

Does a New Mexico LLC still file with the IRS?

Yes. A single-member LLC owned by a non-US person files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year.
