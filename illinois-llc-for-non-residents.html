How to Form an Illinois LLC as a Non-Resident (2026 Cost and Steps)

🏙️📄💵

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: Anyone can form an Illinois LLC from abroad, with no US citizenship, residency or visa. You file Articles of Organization (Form LLC-5.5) with the Illinois Secretary of State for $150, appoint a registered agent in Illinois, then get an EIN from the IRS. Each year you file a $75 annual report before the first day of your anniversary month.

What Illinois charges

  • Articles of Organization (LLC-5.5): $150 with standard processing of about 10 business days, or $250 for 24-hour service.
  • Series LLC articles (LLC-5.5(S)): $400.
  • Annual report (LLC-50.1): $75, plus a penalty if filed late.
  • EIN: free from the IRS.

Step by step

  1. Check the name in the Illinois business search.
  2. Appoint a registered agent. An individual agent must live in Illinois, and a business entity acting as agent must be authorized to do so in Illinois. The registered office must be a physical address in the state.
  3. File the Articles of Organization online or on paper. They include the principal place of business, the registered agent and office, and the names and business addresses of all managers, or of any member with the authority of a manager.
  4. Sign an operating agreement. It is not filed, but banks often ask for it.
  5. Get an EIN with Form SS-4, then open a bank account.

The yearly annual report

The annual report is due before the first day of the LLC's anniversary month, which is the month the LLC was organized. An LLC organized in May 2026 files before May 1, 2027. Late reports carry a penalty, which the Secretary of State says cannot be waived, and an LLC that stays delinquent can be administratively dissolved.

Privacy: what is public

Illinois is less private than Wyoming. The articles list the names and business addresses of the managers or of members with management authority, and these filings are public records. A single-member LLC run by its owner will usually show that owner's name.

Federal steps for non-resident owners

These apply to every US LLC, whatever the state.

  • EIN: without an SSN or ITIN you apply with Form SS-4 by fax or mail. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • Form 5472: a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person files Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year, due April 15, even with no income. See Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs.
  • BOI: LLCs formed in the US, including Illinois LLCs, are exempt from FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting under the final rule effective August 14, 2026. See BOI reporting in 2026.

Registered agent address vs business address

The registered agent address is where the state and courts send legal notices. It must be a physical street address in Illinois. It is not automatically your business or mailing address, and many agents do not forward everyday mail or let you use their address for banking.

Banks and payment providers usually ask for a business address and for each owner's real personal residential address, which for most non-residents is their home abroad. Many banks decline CMRA or virtual mailbox addresses as a business address, so check the bank's eligibility page before you apply. See what a CMRA address is.

Illinois vs Wyoming or Delaware

Illinois costs more than Wyoming to form ($150 vs $100) and to keep ($75 vs a $60 minimum), it lists managers or managing members publicly, and it has state income taxes. It makes sense if you, your team or your operations are in Illinois, since an LLC doing business there would need to register there anyway. For a remote online business, Wyoming is usually simpler and cheaper, and Delaware suits companies planning to raise venture capital. See Wyoming vs Delaware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US citizen form an Illinois LLC?

Yes. Illinois has no citizenship or residency requirement for LLC owners. You need a registered agent with an Illinois address.

How much does an Illinois LLC cost?

The Articles of Organization cost $150 and the annual report costs $75 a year, before registered agent fees.

When is the Illinois annual report due?

Before the first day of the LLC's anniversary month, which is the month it was organized.

Are owner names public for an Illinois LLC?

Often yes. The articles list the names and business addresses of managers or members with management authority.
