Why 73% of Non-US Founders Get Rejected by US Banks

Banks scan the USPS database. If your address is flagged as a Commercial Mail Receiving Agency, you are automatically out of all the personal banking options.

Virtual Mailboxes

Flagged as CMRA. Instant rejection from Stripe, Mercury, and Capital One. Avoid at all costs. Status: Banned.

P.O. Boxes

Banks require a "Physical Street Address." Generic P.O. boxes do not meet Patriot Act requirements. Status: Rejected.

Real Residential Lease

A legal, signed lease for a real residential unit in your personal name, with a verifiable Renter's Insurance. Passes residential address checks and is bank-friendly. Status: 100% Compliant.
