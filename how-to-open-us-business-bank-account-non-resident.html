How to Open a US Business Bank Account as a Non-Resident (2026 Guide)

🏦🌍💳

Last updated November 2026

Short answer: A non-resident can open a US business bank account without travelling, as long as the account is for a US-formed company. You need the LLC's formation documents, an EIN, a passport for every owner, and a US business address that is not a registered agent address, P.O. box or virtual mailbox. Most non-residents open with online banks and fintechs such as Mercury, Relay or Wise Business rather than a branch bank.

What you need before you apply

  • A US LLC or corporation. Banks open accounts for the company, not for you personally. Wyoming and Delaware are the most common states for non-resident founders.
  • An EIN. The company's federal tax ID from the IRS. You can get one without an SSN or ITIN. See how to get an EIN without an SSN.
  • Formation documents. Articles of Organization and, ideally, an operating agreement listing the owners.
  • Passport for each owner with 25% or more ownership, plus anyone who controls the company.
  • The owner's own residential address. This is where you actually live. For most non-residents that is your home address abroad, and online banks that accept non-residents expect that.
  • A US address for the company. This is the one most applications fail on (see below).
  • A clear description of what the business does, its website, and where customers and money will come from.

The two addresses banks ask for

Business account applications ask for two different addresses, and mixing them up is a common reason applications are returned.

The company's address is where the LLC operates or receives mail. Many banks check this address against the USPS list of Commercial Mail Receiving Agencies (CMRAs). Virtual mailboxes, UPS Store boxes and most "virtual office" suites are CMRAs, and registered agent addresses are shared by thousands of companies, so they are often declined for this field. Read what a CMRA address is.

The owner's residential address is your personal home address. Give the address where you really live. Banks verify it against your ID and proof of address, and using an address you do not live at can breach the bank's terms.

Which banks accept non-resident owners

Policies change often, so always check the bank's own eligibility page before applying.

  • Mercury accepts US companies with founders living abroad, provided the company is US-formed and has US operations or plans. It does not accept registered agent addresses, P.O. boxes or virtual mailboxes as the company address, and it does not serve owners from some countries.
  • Relay asks for a US physical operating address for the business.
  • Wise Business opens for US companies with non-US owners and asks for a physical trading address rather than a virtual office.
  • Traditional banks such as Chase or Bank of America usually expect an in-person branch visit, and often a US personal address and SSN or ITIN.

Step by step

  1. Form the LLC and appoint a registered agent.
  2. Get the EIN (fax takes about four business days; mail about four weeks).
  3. Set up a real, non-CMRA US address for the company and keep its documents (lease or service agreement).
  4. Prepare your passport, a recent proof of your home address, and the formation documents.
  5. Apply online, describing the business plainly and consistently with your website.
  6. Answer any follow-up questions from the bank's compliance team quickly and with documents.

Why applications get declined

  • The company address is a CMRA, registered agent or P.O. box.
  • Names or addresses differ between the EIN letter, state filing and application.
  • The business description is vague, or the website is missing or unfinished.
  • The owner lives in a country the bank does not serve.
  • The business is in a high-risk category for that bank.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I open a US business bank account without visiting the US?

Yes. Online banks and fintechs such as Mercury, Relay and Wise Business open accounts for US-formed companies with non-resident owners without a branch visit. Traditional banks usually require an in-person visit.

Do I need an SSN or ITIN to open a US business bank account?

No, not for most online business accounts. The company needs an EIN, and owners verify their identity with a passport. An ITIN helps with personal banking and credit building.

Can I use my registered agent address for my bank account?

Usually not. Registered agent addresses are shared by thousands of companies and many banks decline them as the company's address, along with P.O. boxes and virtual mailboxes.

What residential address should I put on the application?

The address where you actually live. For most non-resident founders that is their home address abroad, which online banks that accept non-residents expect.
